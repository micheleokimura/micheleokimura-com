## Honest notability assessment

Wikipedia's bar for a biography is [WP:GNG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Notability)
plus [WP:BIO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Notability_(people)):
**significant coverage in multiple reliable sources that are independent of the
subject.** Independent is the operative word. It excludes:

- micheleokimura.com, releasinggenerations.org, and explicitmovement.org (self-published)
- press releases, and anything derived from one
- podcast appearances and interviews (the subject is talking, so it is not independent)
- book endorsements, event programmes, and speaker bios
- a subject's own books

Against that test, the current evidence base is:

| Asset | Counts toward notability? |
| ----- | ------------------------- |
| 2023 state Outstanding Advocate award | **Potentially yes**, if independent coverage of it exists. The award itself is a strong signal; right now the only citation is her own organization's site. |
| Hawaiʻi DOE approval of the Hawaiʻi Brave Together booklet | **Potentially yes**, with a DOE document or news report. Strong, unusual, verifiable in principle. |
| Two published trade books | Weak on their own. *Dancing with Father* was published by Xulon Press, a **paid self-publishing imprint**, which Wikipedia treats as self-published. Books need independent reviews to count. |
| Curricula (Brave Series, Dream Big) | Only via the DOE booklet approval. |
| Missio Nexus podcast appearance | **No.** Interviews are not independent coverage. |
| Speaking engagements and endorsements | **No.** |
| Ed Silvoso connection | **Not usable.** See the sourcing note below. |

**Conclusion: one or two more independent secondary sources would likely make
the difference.** The realistic path is press coverage of the DOE booklet
approval or of the 2023 award, in outlets like the Honolulu Star-Advertiser,
Hawaii Business Magazine, Civil Beat, KHON2, or Hawaii News Now. The repo already
identifies these as a target (`research/05-google-knowledge-panel-strategy.md`),
where they are listed as not yet done. Two solid articles and this draft becomes
submittable.

**Do not submit before then.** A declined Articles-for-Creation draft is not
fatal, but a deleted article makes a later attempt harder, and a
[WP:AUTOBIOGRAPHY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Autobiography)
decline attaches to the subject. Michele should not create this article herself.
If she has a connection to whoever submits it, that person must disclose it under
[WP:COI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Conflict_of_interest).

**Wikidata is a different matter and is worth doing now.** See
`WIKIDATA-ENTITY-DRAFT.md`.

**Michele Okimura** is an American author, educator, and nonprofit executive from
Honolulu, Hawaiʻi. She is the founder and executive director of Releasing
Generations, a Hawaiʻi-based nonprofit organization, and the author of youth
curricula used in Hawaiʻi schools and churches.[1][2]

### Early life and career

Okimura was born in Honolulu, Hawaiʻi.[1] She worked for approximately fourteen
years as a public elementary school teacher in Hawaiʻi.[1] In 1997 she and her
husband, Rob Okimura, founded a congregation in Honolulu, originally called Hope
Chapel LifeSpring and later Lifespring Church, where she serves as a part-time
pastor.[1]

> **Editor's note, not for publication.** The repo is internally inconsistent on
> the length of her teaching career: `content/bios/long.md` and `CLAUDE.md` say
> "roughly fourteen years", while `src/lib/credentials.ts` and the live About
> page say seventeen. **Resolve this with Michele before submitting**, and use
> whichever figure the source you cite actually supports.

### Writing

Okimura's first book, *Dancing with Father*, an illustrated collection of poetry
on healing, was published by Xulon Press in 2011.[3] *The Birth of Explicit
Movement* followed in 2018.[2] Two further titles, *Brave Purpose* and *Brave
Purpose with God*, are scheduled for 2027.[2]

She is also the author of several curricula, including the Dream Big Journal
Curriculum, the Raising Kingdom Kids Lesson Book, and the Brave Series, a
teen leadership curriculum comprising *Brave & Beautiful*, *Brave & Bold*, and
*Brave Together*.[2]

The Hawaiʻi Brave Together three-article booklet was reviewed and approved by the
Hawaiʻi Department of Education for the state's Sexual Violence Prevention
Initiative. Its teacher lessons were written by Phyllis Unebasami, a retired
deputy superintendent of the department.[4]

### Recognition

In 2023, at the 30th anniversary of Hawaiʻi Children and Youth Day, Governor Josh
Green and Honolulu Mayor Rick Blangiardi named Okimura the State of Hawaiʻi
Outstanding Advocate for the Children and Youth of Hawaiʻi. The recognition was
given to Okimura together with the Explicit Movement team.[5]

### Organizations

Okimura founded Releasing Generations, which operates the initiatives Explicit
Movement, Kingdom Families, and ReThink Creativity.[1][6] She also runs Michele
Okimura LLC, through which she offers author coaching.[1]

### Bibliography

- *Dancing with Father* (Xulon Press, 2011) ISBN 978-1613792711
- *The Birth of Explicit Movement* (2018)
- *Brave Purpose* (forthcoming, 2027)
- *Brave Purpose with God* (forthcoming, 2027)
