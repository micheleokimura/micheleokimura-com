# Transform Our World Hawaiʻi

## The Organization

Transform Our World is a global movement that gathers Christian leaders around the conviction that the gospel is meant to change cities and regions along with individual lives. It works through local chapters, each one a standing table for the leaders of a particular place. The Hawaiʻi chapter is that table for the islands.

## The Engagement

Michele is part of the Transform Our World Hawaiʻi chapter. The chapter meets weekly and brings together about five senior leaders from across the Missionary Church denomination in Hawaiʻi. This is a membership rather than a booking: Michele sits down with these pastors week after week, in the ordinary rhythm of leaders who carry responsibility for congregations across the state. She also spoke at the Transform Our World Global Conference in October 2022.

## The Outcome

The weekly rhythm is the outcome. Michele's work moves through Hawaiʻi on relationships that are already alive, and this chapter is where a good number of those relationships get their regular maintenance. The leaders around that table are consistent supporters of Releasing Generations.
