# Advance Good

## The Organization

Advance Good is a purpose-driven organization founded by retired Deputy Superintendent Phyllis Unebasami. Dedicated to helping those who want to make a difference, the LLC provides consulting, experience design, and leadership development rooted in clarity, character, and calling. Their mission is to advance good by empowering ideas and people, brilliantly blending strategic thinking with spiritual wisdom to create transformative solutions that honor both excellence and faith.

## The Engagement

Michele is deeply honored to formally partner with Advance Good. She describes Phyllis as a "force of nature" in her beautiful, creative approach to this work.

**Curriculum Development:** As a stellar author and curriculum designer, Phyllis authored the comprehensive curriculum for the Brave Together co-ed version of the Brave series.

**Tailored Solutions:** Through this formal partnership, Michele and Phyllis have successfully created and launched both faith-based and non-faith versions of the Brave Together lessons to ensure they can serve any community.

## The Outcome

Having such a powerful curriculum designer on the team has paved the way for incredible future impact.

**Educator Equipping:** Michele and Advance Good are actively collaborating to provide specialized school and teacher training for the Brave Together curriculum, ensuring schools and organizations have the exact support they need to implement it.

**Global Vision:** The team looks forward to many fruitful years of collaboration ahead, dedicated to promoting and expanding the reach of the Brave series throughout Hawaiʻi, across the United States, and into other countries.
