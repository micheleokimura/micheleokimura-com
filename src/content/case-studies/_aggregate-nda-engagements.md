## Why this framing exists

A real body of work accumulates trust faster than it can be made public. Some of Michele's strongest endorsements come from inside institutions that cannot be named on the public site for institutional reasons. The integrity move is to honor those constraints completely while still giving the public site permission to say, truthfully, that the body of work has been piloted, used, and endorsed inside settings that matter.
