# The Foursquare Church

## The Organization

The Foursquare Church is a global Pentecostal denomination, and Foursquare Missions International (FMI) serves as its dedicated arm for sending and supporting workers around the world. For Michele, this relationship goes far beyond a typical client engagement. She has been actively attending Foursquare since 1992 and is a credentialed Foursquare pastor herself, making this work deeply personal and foundational to her ministry.

## The Engagement

**District Leadership:** The Hawaiʻi Foursquare district, led by District Supervisor Pastor Sheldon Lacsina, is a strong, ongoing supporter of Releasing Generations.

**Keynote Speaking:** In recent years, Michele has served as an invited speaker and workshop leader at district regional leadership conferences, including the Hawaiʻi District Leaders Conference in October 2023.

**Global & Native Outreach:** She delivered keynote addresses at the Arise Native American Camp gathering in Montana during the summers of 2023 and 2026. Additionally, she hosted a booth at the larger global Foursquare gathering, putting her valuable resources directly in front of denominational leaders from around the world.

**Missions Collaboration:** Within the missions arm, Michele's closest working relationship is with Kihāpiʻilani Pimental, Worker Supervisor for Foursquare Missions International. A Native Hawaiian leader with global reach who oversees FMI, Pastor Pimental was present for her sessions in Montana.

Michele recently served as the mission team leader on a Foursquare trip to Hong Kong, where she designed most of the lessons, taught, and oversaw four days of English instruction at a Christian school partnered with Foursquare. The trip marked the very first time a Foursquare national region collaborated with an international region, fulfilling a vision that Foursquare's president, Randy Remington, has nurtured for about five years.

**Curriculum Contributors:** Expanding the reach of the ministry, several Foursquare pastors have contributed articles to the Brave Series, including:

- Senior Pastor and District Supervisor Sheldon Lacsina
- Senior Pastor Brandon Ahu
- Pastor Chad Reyes
- Pastor Brett Nakasone
- Pastor Mark Stinton

## The Outcome

Michele's teaching continues to bring profound, practical transformation to Foursquare leaders, missionaries, and congregations.

Vice President Ted Vail, Senior Vice President of Mission, has been a steady supporter, along with Pastor Kihāpiʻilani Pimental. Both shared the following regarding the deep impact of her work:

Okimura masterfully connects the prophetic and the artistic with the dusty, everyday path we actually walk. This is not a book about avoiding difficulty; it's about finding God's presence and purpose in the midst of it. Readers will come away not just encouraged but awakened as travelers ready to follow God's clues toward the treasure He has prepared."
