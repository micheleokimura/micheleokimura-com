# The Foursquare Church

## The Organization

The Foursquare Church is a global Pentecostal denomination, and Foursquare Missions International is the arm that sends and supports its workers around the world. This is not an outside client for Michele. She has been attending Foursquare since 1986, forty years, and she is a Foursquare pastor herself, which means the work she brings into these rooms comes from inside the house.

## The Engagement

The Hawaiʻi Foursquare district is a strong supporter of Releasing Generations. In recent years Michele has been an invited speaker and workshop leader at the district's regional leadership conferences, including the Hawaiʻi District Leaders Conference in October 2023. She gave the keynote at Arise Camp around 2023, and again at the Arise Native American Camp gathering in Montana in the summer of 2026. She has also held a booth at a larger global Foursquare gathering to put her resources in front of leaders from across the denomination.

Her closest working relationship in the missions arm is with Kihāpiʻilani Pimental, Worker Supervisor for Foursquare Missions International, a Native Hawaiian leader with global reach who oversees FMI. He was in the room in Montana.

## The Outcome

> "She just did a session at Native Camp in Montana and it was excellent, the most impactful session of the whole camp. We had 19 FMI workers there. Every person had an experience of how to prophesy over each other. Simple, practical, and powerful. I saw it all personally. Now many children in our church prophesy and unashamedly pray for healing, all because of Michele."
> Pastor Kihāpiʻilani Pimental, Worker Supervisor, Foursquare Missions International

The last line is the one that matters most. The session did not stop with the nineteen workers who attended. It reached the children in their churches. Michele's friendships with Foursquare's national and global leadership continue to grow.
