# Hawaiʻi State Department of Education

## The Organization

The Hawaiʻi State Department of Education runs public education for the whole state as a single statewide district, serving students on every major island. Because Hawaiʻi is one district rather than many, a curriculum decision made at the department level reaches schools across the state at once. That structure is what makes department approval matter so much for any program written for young people.

## The Engagement

Michele brought Brave Together, the third curriculum in her Brave Series, to the department for review. Phyllis Unebasami, the department's former Deputy Superintendent, carried the approval through. Brave Together comes out of the same body of work as Brave & Beautiful and Brave & Bold: material written to help young people talk honestly with each other about identity, worth, and courage, in a format a teacher or counselor can pick up and run.

## The Outcome

Brave Together now carries Hawaiʻi State DOE approval. By July 2026 Michele was describing it publicly as newly DOE-approved. For public school counselors, advisors, and classroom teachers across the state, that approval means the material arrives already cleared, so nobody has to build the case for it from scratch before they can use it with students.
