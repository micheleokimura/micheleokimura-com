# State of Hawaiʻi Department of Education

## The Organization

The Hawaiʻi State Department of Education operates as a single, statewide public school district serving students across every major island. Because it functions as one unified district, curriculum decisions made at the department level can effectively reach and impact schools throughout the entire state.

## The Engagement

Our working relationship with the department was carefully built over the course of two years.

**Initial Connection:** In September 2024, Michele met with recently retired Deputy Superintendent Phyllis Unebasami, who immediately championed bringing the Brave Series to Hawaiʻi's public secondary schools.

**Curriculum Development:** Phyllis joined the Brave Series team to expertly design the Brave Together Coed Teacher Lessons, ensuring the curriculum was perfectly tailored for easy public school implementation.

**Leadership Support:** In December 2024, Phyllis and Michele presented the program to Superintendent Hayashi, who shared their vision of making it accessible to secondary schools statewide.

**Statewide Outreach:** Over the following year, the team finalized the coed version of the curriculum. Michele and Phyllis Unebasami went on to present the Brave Series at a statewide meeting for Hawaiʻi middle school principals, and later at a Maui meeting for complex counselors.

## The Outcome

In the summer of 2026, after a thorough vetting process and collaboration with the DOE, the partnership yielded positive results.

**State Approval:** The team created a customized, 3-article Hawaiʻi version of Brave Together, which the DOE approved for use in secondary schools to meet the state's Sexual Violence Prevention Initiative mandate.

**Resource Distribution:** The complex leader overseeing the Maui meeting purchased Dream Big Journals for every counselor in attendance.
