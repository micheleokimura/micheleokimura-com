## The school

Sacred Hearts Academy is an independent Catholic college-preparatory school for girls in Honolulu, with roots stretching back to 1909. The school is led by President Dr. Brandy Ann Sato, PhD. The Sacred Hearts community sits inside the network of Hawai'i independent and faith-rooted schools that Michele's curriculum and speaking work has been moving through.

## How the introduction came

In March 2026, Gerald Teramae, Head of School at Island Pacific Academy and a peer in the Hawai'i private-school heads-of-school network, wrote to introduce Michele to Dr. Brandy Ann Sato. The note carried the warmth of a Hawai'i private-school referral. Michele wrote to Dr. Sato on the same thread, offering to share more about the Brave Series work and to explore possibilities of serving the Sacred Hearts community.

Within days, Dr. Sato wrote back to schedule a meeting. Shyann Fermahin from the Sacred Hearts team handled the logistics. The meeting landed on the Sacred Hearts campus on Tuesday, March 31, 2026.

## The work the partnership is exploring

The Sacred Hearts conversation is about whether the Brave Series can serve the Sacred Hearts student community. Michele's followup to Dr. Sato, the day after their meeting, named the visit as "a joy and a profound blessing" and offered the next steps Sacred Hearts would need in order to evaluate the work for the school community.

The conversation continued through Spring 2026. As of June 2026, a followup meeting is scheduled for the last week of June, with the Sacred Hearts team and Michele working through the calendar around the school's senior graduation and end-of-year cycle.

## Why this page exists already

The Sacred Hearts conversation is documented here because the partnership architecture matters even when the engagement has not yet shipped a program. The trajectory from a peer referral, to a campus meeting with the school president, to a real follow-up cycle is the way credible faith-school partnerships actually come together. When the engagement converts into a delivered program, this page picks up where it left off and ships.

If you lead a faith-rooted school and want the Brave Series brought into a real conversation about your community, the path Sacred Hearts is walking is the path the conversation usually takes. Start with a note.
