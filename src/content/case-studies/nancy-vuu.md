# Nancy Vuu

## The Organization

Nancy Vuu is one of Michele's collaborators in Hawaiʻi's youth and community work.

## The Engagement

Michele and Nancy co-led the engagement at Kupu Center, the community-based organization serving high-risk youth in Hawaiʻi. The two of them spoke together to the young people there about dreaming big for their lives, and Michele used the visit to introduce Brave & Beautiful and Brave & Bold to the Kupu Center leadership team. Michele has also written an endorsement for Nancy.

## The Outcome

The partnership at Kupu Center opened a door that is still open: a senior leader there is planning Brave & Bold for the boys and weighing Brave & Beautiful for the girls. See the [Kupu Center case study](/case-studies/kupu-center) for the fuller account of that engagement.
