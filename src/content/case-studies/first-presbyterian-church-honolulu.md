# First Presbyterian Church of Honolulu

## When a new nonprofit found its first mentor

When Michele first launched Releasing Generations, Senior Pastor Dan Chun, who led First Presbyterian Church of Honolulu from 1994 to 2023, reached out with a simple offer: how could he help. That question opened a relationship that has now spanned three decades and is still going.

Senior Pastor Dan met with Michele and her early team a handful of times in those first years, offering the kind of practical wisdom that only comes from someone who has already walked the road. He asked about their governance, their calling, and their sustainability. He listened more than he spoke. When the formal meetings ended, the warm, informal mentorship kept going. Pastor Dan has remained a friend, sounding board, and quiet encourager to Michele across the entire life of the nonprofit.

His imprint reaches beyond his own congregation. Dan and Pam Chun founded Hawaiian Islands Ministries (HIM) in 1983, with a vision of catalyzing Christian renewal and revival in Hawaiʻi through a trans-denominational, cross-generational equipping ministry for churches and leaders across the islands. HIM has been shaping Hawaiʻi's Christian landscape for over forty years, and it is that same equipping heart Pastor Dan brought to his conversations with Michele.

In the fall of 2025, Pastor Dan joined the Explicit Movement speaker team, bringing decades of pastoral wisdom to a new generation of leaders.

## A women's small group that changed the audience for Brave

More recently, Leona Larkin, First Presbyterian's Care and Restoration Ministries Director, and Michele co-led a women leaders' small group through the Brave & Beautiful curriculum, part of the Brave Series. The Brave Series was originally written for teenage girls. What surprised everyone was how deeply the material landed with the adult women in that group. The conversations were unguarded, the transformation was visible, and the framework proved just as fitting for women navigating leadership as it had been for young women navigating adolescence.

The response led to something Michele had never planned for. In 2026, First Presbyterian Church of Honolulu purchased the Brave & Bold curriculum to mentor and equip its men leaders, extending what the women had discovered into the men's ministry too.

## A partnership Michele holds with real gratitude

Michele considers it a genuine honor to work with Leona Larkin and to support the work of First Presbyterian Church of Honolulu. What began three decades ago as one senior pastor's offer to help a new nonprofit find its footing has become a partnership in equipping leaders across generations. That is the kind of relationship you build once and keep for life.
