# Hawaiʻi Baptist Academy

## The Organization

Hawaiʻi Baptist Academy (HBA) is a K-12 Christian school in Honolulu, serving approximately 1,100 students from preschool through twelfth grade on its Oʻahu campus. Identity formation is a deliberate thread woven throughout their academic year, highlighted by events like their dedicated ReFocus day, designed to bring the entire school community into a posture of reflection, faith, and renewal. HBA's leadership team prioritizes depth, actively seeking profound content that the school can return to and utilize for professional development long after guest speakers leave the stage.

## The Engagement

What began as single speaking invitations has grown into a multi-year, ongoing partnership that impacts students, parents, and the full faculty and staff.

**Christian Emphasis Week:** Michele and the Releasing Generations team have led multiple assemblies for both middle and high school students.

**Parent Workshops:** Michele has guided several workshops for middle school parents, equipping them with the language to discuss identity, worth, and the real pressures their children face.

**Brave & Beautiful Pilot:** Michele led a weekly lunch-period pilot group for secondary-aged girls, working through the entire set of *Brave & Beautiful* volumes to give the material a real classroom test with the exact demographic it was written for.

**ReFocus Day & K-12 Teacher Training:** In January 2026, Michele and her teaching partner Joshua Kaina spoke to the HBA community for ReFocus day, drawing on the Brave curriculum, Explicit Movement teachings, and the testimony-driven work she co-authors with her husband, Rob. Later that month, Michele and her team trained HBA's entire teaching staff of about 150 educators. The content resonated so deeply that the school requested to keep the presentation slides for internal professional development, a request confirmed in writing by HBA's Tony Traughber.

## The Outcome

The most honest measure of a school's engagement is whether the institution wants the content to stay. HBA leadership successfully used the handouts Michele's team created as the foundation for their next two teacher trainings, and the administration is now exploring ways to implement the *Brave Together* curriculum following the successful girls' pilot.
