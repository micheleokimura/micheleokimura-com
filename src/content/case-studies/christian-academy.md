# Christian Academy

## The Organization

Christian Academy is the school of First Assembly of God in Honolulu. It is a campus where faith formation and academics are held together rather than kept in separate rooms, and where the leadership has been willing to let student ministry go further than most schools are comfortable with.

## The Engagement

Michele has worked with Christian Academy as a paying client, with invoices on file for the material and the sessions. The relationship has kept climbing the org chart: her main contact is Ninia Richardson, and in July 2026 Michele led a leadership Zoom for the school and gave a presentation to the board. Her content on campus has centered on the Kingdom Kids material, teaching children and the adults around them to hear God and pray for each other with confidence.

## The Outcome

Rebecca Furuhashi, the school's Principal, on what the work did to the campus:

> "Her creative and visually-exciting presentations have challenged us and our students to directly download from the Father Heart of God. We are now seeing children as young as five praying bold and encouraging words over other children and even their teachers. Our entire campus culture has changed. Our school will never be the same."
> Rebecca Furuhashi, Principal, Christian Academy

The board presentation and the leadership Zoom in July 2026 mean the conversation has moved from individual sessions to what the school wants to build.
