# Hawaiʻi Catholic Schools

## The Organization

Hawaiʻi Catholic Schools is the network of Catholic schools across the islands, and its annual educators conference is the one gathering that brings teachers and administrators from those campuses into a single room.

## The Engagement

Michele was invited to the Catholic Schools Educators Annual Conference in Hawaiʻi in February 2024, where she delivered both a keynote and a workshop. The format matters: a keynote reaches the room, and a workshop sends people home with something they can run on Monday.

## The Outcome

The engagement put Michele's work in front of educators from Catholic campuses across the state, widening the reach of the Brave and Dream Big material into a school network beyond the independent and evangelical schools where it started.
