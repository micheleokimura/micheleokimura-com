# Island Pacific Academy

## The Organization

Island Pacific Academy is an independent school on Oʻahu, led by Head of School Gerald Teramae. Like many of Hawaiʻi's independent schools, IPA carries a formation program alongside its academics, and its leadership team takes seriously the question of what the adults on campus model for students.

## The Engagement

Michele and Gerald Teramae have been friends for close to fifteen years, and they have collaborated on many occasions across that stretch. The anchor piece of work came when Michele presented the Dream Big Journal to IPA's leadership team at their leadership retreat. She started with the adults, asking the leaders to revisit their own dreams before asking anything of their students. Gerald wrote a formal endorsement of that presentation afterward.

The two of them keep meeting to envision future events together, and IPA currently has Brave Series samples in hand that the school is evaluating for its own use.

## The Outcome

Gerald Teramae, on the leadership-team session:

> "Michele was able to rekindle and inspire the need and desire to reignite and inspire our Leadership Team that it is never too late to become a 'dreamer' and make a positive impact and difference in the world."
> Gerald Teramae, Head of School, Island Pacific Academy

He has also become one of the most active introducers of Michele's work inside Hawaiʻi's private-school network, including the referral that opened the door at Sacred Hearts Academy. When Brave & Bold was completed in May 2026, he wrote: "Congratulations Michele! We are so proud of you. Keep up the good work. Is the series offered in Japanese and Korean?"
