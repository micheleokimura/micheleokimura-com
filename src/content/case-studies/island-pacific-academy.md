# Island Pacific Academy

## The Organization

Island Pacific Academy is an independent school on Oʻahu, led by Head of School Gerald Teramae. Like many of Hawaiʻi's independent schools, IPA carries a formation program alongside its academics, and its leadership team takes seriously the question of what the adults on campus model for students.

## The Engagement

The relationship started with the adults. Michele brought the Dream Big Journal work to IPA's leadership team, who used it to revisit their own dreams and aspirations before asking anything of their students. Gerald Teramae wrote a testimonial about the Journal in 2024, and in February 2026 he followed it with a full formal endorsement of the Dream Big book. Meanwhile Steve Ross has been actively wiring the Brave Series into IPA's Advisory curriculum, the school's structured space for exactly this kind of work with students.

## The Outcome

IPA has moved from a single session to a curriculum decision, which is the harder and more meaningful of the two. Gerald Teramae, on the leadership-team session:

> "Michele was able to rekindle and inspire the need and desire to reignite and inspire our Leadership Team that it is never too late to become a 'dreamer' and make a positive impact and difference in the world."
> Gerald Teramae, Head of School, Island Pacific Academy

The relationship is ongoing, and the Advisory curriculum work continues.
