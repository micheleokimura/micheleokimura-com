# Press Kit and Speaker Page Brief

Reference document for the micheleokimura.com Speaker page and downloadable press kit.
Compiled 2026-08-21.

## Executive summary

The 2026 professional standard for a speaker press kit is a single downloadable PDF that a meeting planner can forward to a booking committee in under 30 seconds, backed by a live Speaker page on the site where the same story is told with video and current dates. Every reputable source converges on the same required components: a stage photo plus clean headshot, an audience-impact headline, bios at three lengths, two to four titled talks with takeaways, visible proof (logos, testimonial, notable stages), and one clear booking contact. The Speaker page above the fold must answer three questions on load (who she is, who she serves, what to do next) and lead with a video reel, because planners rank video higher than any other asset when evaluating fit. For Michele, the strategic move is to run one press kit with a modular faith paragraph that can be swapped for secular audiences, and to lead the Speaker page with the four-hats identity so bookers immediately see author, speaker, executive director, and coach in a single frame. Hawaii is a differentiator worth surfacing early, both as origin story and as a workshop and retreat destination.

## Recommended press kit components (in order)

1. **Cover page with stage photo and audience-impact headline.** Planners are picturing Michele in front of their audience; the stage shot does that work before any copy has to.
2. **Positioning statement (one sentence, under 20 words).** Answers "who is this for and what changes" so the committee has a line they can quote to a boss.
3. **Short bio (about 50 words).** For event apps, intros from the podium, and social captions. Name, current role, one standout credential.
4. **Medium bio (about 150 words).** The most commonly requested length; goes in conference brochures and podcast show notes.
5. **Long bio (about 400 words).** For speaker-page profiles and press use; carries the full four-hats arc and the 2023 State of Hawai'i award.
6. **Signature talks (three to four titled keynotes/workshops).** Each with title, one-sentence hook, three-bullet audience takeaways, and format options (keynote, half-day workshop, retreat). Titled talks book better than a topic list.
7. **Audience and formats grid.** Small table showing who she serves (schools, faith communities, women's leadership, family and community groups) crossed with formats (keynote, workshop, retreat, panel). Lets a planner locate themselves in five seconds.
8. **Proof block: client and venue logos.** Six to twelve logos in a clean grid; this is what gets forwarded up the chain.
9. **Testimonial (one strong pull-quote, attributed).** Lead with the Gerald Teramae (Island Pacific Academy) endorsement. Add a second short quote if space allows.
10. **Books and works panel.** Cover thumbnails for *Dancing With Father*, *The Birth of Explicit Movement*, the Dream Big Journals, and the Brave Series, plus a *Brave Purpose* 2027 forward-marker. Establishes authored-body-of-work authority.
11. **Media and press mentions (if any).** Podcasts, TV, awards. The 2023 Outstanding Advocate for Children and Youth award goes here.
12. **Photo library reference sheet.** Thumbnail contact-sheet of downloadable photos (three stage, two headshot, one lifestyle), each with resolution and orientation labeled.
13. **Technical rider (light).** AV needs, travel origin (Honolulu), preferred session length. Keeps back-and-forth off email.
14. **Booking contact block.** One name, one email, one phone, website URL, and a link back to the online inquiry form. No committee routing.
15. **Back cover with QR code to the Speaker page.** So a printed copy in a planner's hand still leads back to the live hub.

Target length: 6 to 8 pages, single PDF, letter and A4 versions, under 5 MB.

## Recommended Speaker page structure (top to bottom)

1. **Hero.** Full-bleed stage photo. Headline built on audience impact ("Help your people remember how to dream"), one-sentence subhead naming the four audiences, two CTAs side by side: **Inquire about booking** (primary) and **Watch the reel** (secondary). Small logo bar under the fold-line as proof-on-load.
2. **Speaker reel.** 90-second sizzle video embedded, autoplay muted with captions. Planners watch before they read.
3. **Who Michele speaks to.** Four short cards mapping to the four hats and the three brand pillars (Organization / Family and Community / Personal Journey), with an added row for faith audiences.
4. **Signature talks.** Same three or four titled programs as the press kit, each expandable. Title, hook, takeaways, formats, ideal audience.
5. **Recent and upcoming engagements.** Logo grid of past venues (Island Pacific Academy, K2KEd, Miami Women of Influence, New Hope Hilo, Dream Expo Hawai'i) plus a running list of upcoming dates.
6. **Testimonial wall.** Two or three quote cards with photo, name, title, and organization. Teramae quote first.
7. **About Michele (medium bio).** The 150-word version, with the four-hats framing and the Hawai'i origin surfaced. Link to the full About page.
8. **Books and body of work.** Cover row with short caption per title. This is the case-study stack.
9. **Press kit download.** Large card: "Download the press kit (PDF, X MB)" plus links to individual assets (long bio, photos zip, logos zip). Duplicate the primary Inquire CTA here.
10. **Booking inquiry form.** Under 90 seconds to complete. Fields: name, organization, event date, event location, audience size, format, budget range, brief description. CTA button reads **Send booking inquiry**, not "Submit."
11. **Footer.** Same booking email, socials, quick links to About, Books, Coaching, Blog.

## Michele-specific notes

**Bridging secular and faith audiences.** Run one press kit as the default, with a single labeled "For faith audiences" paragraph in the long bio that adds the pastoral role, Lifespring Church, and the Releasing Generations mission line. Secular version omits that paragraph and leads with the State of Hawai'i award and the Brave Series curriculum work in schools. Do not make two separate PDFs unless a bureau requests it; a modular one-pager avoids voice drift. On the Speaker page, keep the hero universal and give faith framing its own card in the "Who Michele speaks to" section, so faith visitors see themselves without secular planners feeling boxed out.

**Hawai'i as differentiator.** Surface Honolulu-based in the medium and long bios and in the retreats context. Hawai'i is a genuine draw for destination retreats and a credibility marker for her educator work with local schools. Use it as origin story, not as decoration.

**Four hats without flattening.** Both the press kit long bio and the Speaker page About block must land all four hats (author, speaker, executive director, coach) in the same paragraph, in that order. Bookers who only need the speaker slice will still see the authored body of work behind it, which is her authority stack while the Brave Purpose Author Method coaching practice is early.

**Books as case studies.** Because coaching testimonials are thin at this stage, the books-and-works panel in the press kit is doing real proof work. Do not shrink it; give it its own page.

**Photo strategy.** Michele has a substantial stage-photo archive in the Drive. Curate three stage shots (wide, medium, close), two headshots (color, neutral background), and one warm environmental portrait. Deliver all at 300 DPI, minimum 2000x2500 pixels, JPEG, plus web-optimized versions.

**Voice guard.** The kit ships without em dashes, without "X, not Y" constructions, without any of the flagged AI-tell vocabulary. Sentence rhythm follows her own writing: warm, second-person where appropriate, medium sentences with short percussive ones for emphasis. Michele. One L.
