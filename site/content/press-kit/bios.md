## Long (~300 words)

Michele Okimura is an author, speaker, workshop leader, executive director, and coach. She is the founder of Michele Okimura LLC and the founder and Executive Director of Releasing Generations, a Christian nonprofit.

Honolulu-born, Michele lives on O'ahu, Hawai'i. She spent roughly fourteen years as a Hawai'i public school teacher and is a part-time pastor at Lifespring Church in Honolulu, the church she and her husband Rob planted in 1997 (originally as Hope Chapel LifeSpring). Her three vantage points (classroom teacher, pastor, mother) shape how she works with adults stepping into a deeper purpose.

In 2023, Governor Josh Green and Mayor Rick Blangiardi named Michele the State of Hawai'i Outstanding Advocate for Children and Youth. The award was conferred at the 30th anniversary of Hawai'i Children and Youth Day.

Michele is the author of *Dancing with Father* (2011), *The Birth of Explicit Movement* (2018), and the forthcoming *Brave Purpose* and *Brave Purpose with God* (2027). She created the Dream Big Journal Curriculum (preschool through adult editions), the Raising Kingdom Kids Lesson Book, and the Brave Series teen leadership curriculum (Brave & Beautiful, Brave & Bold, Brave Together). Her central methodology, the Dream Big Framework, runs across her writing, her teaching, and her coaching.

The LLC is Michele's commercial vehicle: workshops, retreats, coaching engagements, the Wisdom Flows blog, and the Brave Purpose Author Method (her six-month one-to-one author coaching engagement). Releasing Generations, her nonprofit, houses the programs closest to her ministry calling: Explicit Movement, Kingdom Families, ReThink Creativity, and the Brave Series.

Michele has taught in classrooms and on stages in Hawai'i, the mainland U.S., the Philippines, and Singapore. She speaks credibly across faith and secular audiences. Her voice is warm, hopeful, mentor-toned, and story-anchored. Her work is grounded in the conviction that healing is the foundation for hope, and that brave purpose is available to anyone willing to walk the road.
