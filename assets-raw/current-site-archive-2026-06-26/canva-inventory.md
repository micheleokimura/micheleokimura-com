## Honest limits of the Canva MCP

The Canva MCP I have access to can:
- Search designs by title/text content
- List folders and their items
- Read design text content (rich text)
- Get design thumbnails
- Export designs as PDF, JPG, PNG, PPTX, GIF, MP4, CSV

The Canva MCP CANNOT:
- Search inside the Uploads folder by filename keyword (only sort alphabetically and paginate, which would require many calls to scan 1000+ images)
- Run image recognition or visual search over uploads
- Export individual uploaded images (only whole designs)
- Identify which design an upload is embedded in

This is why I cannot say with confidence whether the missing photos are or are not somewhere in the Uploads folder. They might be there with hash filenames, but locating them programmatically isn't supported.
