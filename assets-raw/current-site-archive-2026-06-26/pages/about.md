## About Michele

## Welcome.

I'm an author and speaker who has spent the last four decades inspiring courage, vulnerability, healing, and creativity.

[Instagram] -> https://www.instagram.com/michele_okimura/

---

### Maybe dreams give purpose a voice.

My story has been an adventure in finding purpose, and it began with my own healing journey. Transforming feelings of worthlessness into a deep sense of value ignited a fire in me-a passion to help others discover the same strength within themselves.

Here's the official part: I'm an author, speaker, and chairman of Releasing Generations, a non-profit that empowers children, youth, and adults through workshops, retreats, and creative sessions. I was awarded the "Outstanding Advocate for the Children and Youth in the State of Hawaii" award in 2023 by Hawaii's Governor Green and Mayor Blangiardi. I've written multiple books and curricula on creativity, inner healing, and the empowerment of future generations.

For 15 years, I was an elementary school teacher, and for 23 years, I served as a pastor alongside my husband, which gave me a profound compassion for people from all walks of life.

I've poured my heart into my books to share the keys I've discovered along the way: from my healing journey in ***Dancing with Father*,** to pursuing big dreams in ***The Birth of Explicit Movement: Discover Keys to Fulfilling Your Purpose,*** *a*nd empowering the next generation with the ***Dream Big Journal*** and the ***Brave Series*** *curricula*.

Everything I do is focused on helping others flourish in life and live out loud to their fullest potential.

Established in 2017, Michele Okimura Consulting has expanded my capacity to live my purpose through the power of writing and by equipping others through workshops, conferences, retreats, and coaching.

**My firm belief** is that every person's story-even the broken ones-can become a launchpad into a future full of freedom, wonder, and limitless possibilities.

When I'm not writing, you'll find me with my two amazing "twin-like" kids, Aaron and Jessica, or gardening with my husband, Rob, my best friend and the love of my life.
