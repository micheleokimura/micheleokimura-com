# Dreaming Big

Welcome to My First Official Blog

What a joy and honor it is to welcome you here. Thank you for joining me on this journey. As I sat with my thoughts, wondering where to begin, I felt it only fitting to start at the beginning-my own.

Beauty from ashes.

It's a phrase I hold close, a quiet anthem that speaks to the possibility of transformation in any season of life.

Once, there was a little girl-afraid, overlooked, and often hidden away in the safety of a dark closet. She felt unloved, uncertain, and lost. But slowly, through faith and the quiet resilience that only time can reveal, she grew into a woman who cherishes life, who believes she has worth, dreams, and a purpose.

That girl was me.

Even now, this truth brings tears to my eyes. I've lived the miracle of beauty rising from ashes. And some of the greatest miracles, I've found, are the ones that take place within the heart.

So here we are. I invite you to walk with me through reflections, stories, wonderings, and hard-won wisdom. My hope is that something here speaks to your own journey-that you find courage, insight, and a little more light for the path ahead.

And truly, I'll be so blessed to have your company as we piece together the meaning in our moments, and discover the stories waiting to be told.

Let's step into the river of the unknown-where wisdom flows.
