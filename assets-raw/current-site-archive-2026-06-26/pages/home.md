## About Michele Okimura

## Do You Believe You're a Creative Genius?

You are a creator. That spark is not confined to a canvas or a stage; it flows through the way you solve a problem, mend a relationship, or find a new path forward. For years, I forgot how to dream. I believed my past defined my limits. My journey taught me that healing is the foundation for hope. My purpose is to help you dream bigger for every facet of your life. Your dreams have the power to change the world.
