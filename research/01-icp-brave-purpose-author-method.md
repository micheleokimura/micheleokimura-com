## The single-sentence ICP

A mid-life Christian leader (pastor, educator, ministry founder, or long-serving lay leader), typically age 45-70, who has carried a book in them for years but has never had the structure or the coach to finish it, and who wants a warm mentor-led engagement that walks with them from wherever they are to a publication-ready manuscript in their own voice.

## The four starting beliefs the Method dismantles

Michele's Method is built to address four beliefs that block would-be authors from finishing. Understanding these beliefs is central to marketing the offering.

### 1. "I cannot write."

The reader believes they lack the writing craft. The Method addresses this by naming that voice matters more than craft and by using the client's own spoken voice as the source material.

### 2. "Who am I to write this?"

The reader believes they lack the credential to speak. The Method addresses this by starting with the origin story: what the reader has lived is the credential.

### 3. "My story is not compelling."

The reader believes no one will care. The Method addresses this by showing the reader that the shape of a compelling story is a structure, and structure can be learned in six months.

### 4. "I cannot write well enough."

The reader believes AI or a ghostwriter would produce a better book. The Method addresses this by teaching the voice-corpus method: the reader's voice, refined and drafted with AI as a working partner, produces a book that reads like the reader on the page.

## Journey stages

### Awareness (first 60 days of interaction)

Trigger: Google search, podcast mention, book back-cover CTA, or a friend's referral. Awareness content: Michele's About page, Wisdom Flows blog, and the FAQ. Awareness CTA: subscribe to Wisdom Flows.

### Consideration (60 days to 6 months)

Trigger: reading a hero blog post, watching a keynote, hearing Michele on a podcast. Consideration content: The Brave Purpose Author Method page, the /faq, the /works page (case studies of Michele's authored body of work). Consideration CTA: book a free 45-minute Session 0.

### Decision (6 months to 12 months)

Trigger: Session 0 call went well. Reader is ready to sign. Decision content: HoneyBook proposal + smart file, signed contract, first invoice paid, onboarding email. Decision CTA: signed contract, $2,498.50 deposit.

### Retention and referral (during and after engagement)

Trigger: writer finishes Week 26. Retention content: post-engagement follow-up notes at day 7, 30, 90, and 180. Referral CTA: a note asking who else the writer is thinking of.
