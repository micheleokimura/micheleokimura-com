# Keyword research: The Brave Purpose Author Method

**Purpose:** Keyword clusters and topic pillars for the site and the editorial calendar. Ranks head, long-tail, question, local, and faith-specific terms by estimated intent, difficulty, and fit with Michele's authority.

**Date:** 2026-07-01

---

## Methodology

Keywords below are grouped by cluster. Each cluster maps to a page or a group of related pages on the site. Every keyword has a rough classification:

- **Intent:** Informational (I) / Commercial (C) / Transactional (T) / Navigational (N)
- **Difficulty:** Low (L) / Medium (M) / High (H). Difficulty here means "how crowded is the SERP with high-authority competitors."
- **Volume estimate:** Rough monthly search volume band based on general knowledge of the space. Verify with a Semrush or Ahrefs pass at launch.

Michele's authority score is currently low (0 backlinks, new domain reputation once the WordPress site is decommissioned). Long-tail and question keywords are her fastest wins.
