# Competitor analysis: Christian author coaching / memoir coaching / book coaching

**Purpose:** Name the players. Understand their positioning. Locate where Michele differentiates.

**Date:** 2026-07-01

---

## Methodology

Competitors identified from the general market of book coaching, with emphasis on Christian and memoir-adjacent sub-niches. Pricing where public. Where pricing is quote-only, noted as such. Positioning summary is from each competitor's homepage messaging.

**Note:** Competitor analysis at launch should be refreshed. The below is a working map, not a market study. A quarterly refresh is recommended.

---

## Direct competitors (Christian book coaching)

### 1. Author Learning Center / Bible-based coaches

- **Positioning:** General Christian authorship guidance.
- **Format:** Various one-off webinars, groups.
- **Pricing:** Ranges $500-$3,000 for coaching packages.
- **Strengths:** Broad reach. Established distribution.
- **Weaknesses:** No signature methodology visible. Cookie-cutter feel. No named coach with the credential stack Michele carries.

### 2. Ann Kroeker (bookcoachingcertification.com)

- **Positioning:** Established book coach, also runs a certification program for other book coaches.
- **Format:** Individual coaching + group programs.
- **Pricing:** Book coaching $500-$5,000+ depending on package.
- **Strengths:** Long track record. Podcasting presence.
- **Weaknesses:** Register skews more secular / craft-focused; not the primary pick for Christian memoir writers.

### 3. Jerry B. Jenkins Writers Guild

- **Positioning:** "Learn from a bestselling author." Christian-native. Jerry Jenkins wrote the *Left Behind* series.
- **Format:** Membership community + courses.
- **Pricing:** $497/year membership; individual coaching packages higher.
- **Strengths:** Massive brand. Deep Christian audience.
- **Weaknesses:** Membership-community format. Not a one-to-one coaching engagement. Focus on fiction more than memoir.

### 4. Christian Writers Institute

- **Positioning:** Christian-native craft education for writers.
- **Format:** Courses + workshops.
- **Pricing:** Course-based; individual courses $50-$300.
- **Strengths:** Long-standing brand.
- **Weaknesses:** Course-based, not coaching. No one-to-one relationship.

### 5. Susy Flory (Called Christian Writers Retreat)

- **Positioning:** Christian nonfiction author, agent-adjacent, runs retreats and mentorship.
- **Format:** Retreats + coaching.
- **Pricing:** Retreats $2,000-$4,000. Coaching quote-only.
- **Strengths:** Established Christian nonfiction credentials. Agent relationships.
- **Weaknesses:** Focus on secular-facing nonfiction; less signature-methodology positioning.

### 6. Kathy Peel / Family Manager

- **Positioning:** Christian speaker and author coach with a family-and-parenting lens.
- **Format:** Speaking + coaching.
- **Pricing:** Quote-only.
- **Strengths:** Established Christian brand.
- **Weaknesses:** Not primarily positioned as a book coach.

## Direct competitors (secular memoir coaching)

### 7. Jennie Nash (Author Accelerator)

- **Positioning:** The best-known memoir coaching brand in the U.S. Author Accelerator trains and certifies book coaches; Jennie is the founder.
- **Format:** Individual coaching + certification program for other coaches.
- **Pricing:** Individual coaching packages $5,000-$15,000+. Certification $3,000-$7,000.
- **Strengths:** Category-defining brand. Deep methodology.
- **Weaknesses:** Secular-facing. Christian writers are not the primary audience. Coaches are certified but assigned; the buyer is not necessarily working with Jennie directly.

### 8. Marion Roach Smith (The Memoir Project)

- **Positioning:** Established memoir craft teacher and NPR contributor.
- **Format:** Group courses + individual consulting.
- **Pricing:** Courses $250-$1,500. Individual work quote-only.
- **Strengths:** Deep craft. Strong media credentials.
- **Weaknesses:** Secular. Craft-first; less transformation-focused.

### 9. Beth Kempton (Do What You Love)

- **Positioning:** UK-based memoir coach and Japan-culture author. Sits adjacent to the personal-development / memoir intersection Michele occupies.
- **Format:** Courses + retreats + coaching.
- **Pricing:** Courses $200-$500. Retreats $2,000-$5,000. 1:1 coaching quote-only.
- **Strengths:** Warm register. Strong community.
- **Weaknesses:** UK-based; timezone friction for U.S. clients.

## Adjacent competitors (AI + writing)

### 10. Writing with AI courses (various)

- **Positioning:** How-to-use-ChatGPT-and-Claude-for-writing content proliferating fast.
- **Format:** Video courses, PDF guides, YouTube channels.
- **Pricing:** $50-$500 typical.
- **Strengths:** Cheap. Volume.
- **Weaknesses:** No coach in the room. No voice-preservation methodology. Almost all secular. Michele's Christian voice-corpus positioning is unoccupied.

### 11. Traditional developmental editors + book coaches offering AI consultation

- **Positioning:** Established coaches adding AI to their existing offering.
- **Format:** 1:1 coaching + AI training bolt-on.
- **Pricing:** Ranges wildly, $2,000-$25,000.
- **Strengths:** Established credibility.
- **Weaknesses:** AI often feels retrofitted; not their native methodology.

## Adjacent competitors (Christian speaking + authorship platforms)

### 12. Suzanne Eller / Proverbs 31 Ministries

- **Positioning:** Christian devotional author with community and mentorship offerings.
- **Format:** Community + occasional coaching.
- **Pricing:** Community $50-$300/year; individual coaching quote-only.
- **Strengths:** Large Christian audience.
- **Weaknesses:** Not a signature book-coaching methodology.

## Michele's differentiation stack

The following five differentiators do not appear in combination on any of the competitors above.

### 1. The Christian-native + AI-forward combination

The Christian book coaching market has almost no incumbent offering AI-assisted coaching. The secular AI-writing space has almost no incumbent offering Christian-native voice work. Michele occupies both. Her voice-corpus methodology is native to both.

### 2. The Hawaii-native identity

Michele is Hawaii-born, Hawaii-based, and holds a state-level award. No other Christian book coach carries this identity. It is a real trust signal for Hawaii-diaspora buyers (a real audience) and a differentiation signal for mainland buyers who value place-anchored work.

### 3. The credential stack

Michele combines: 20+ years pastoral (Hope Chapel / Foursquare lineage), 14 years public school teaching, published-author status with two more books coming, state-level award (2023), nonprofit executive director, and speaker with international reach. Almost no competitor has all five.

### 4. The four-belief methodology

Michele's Method dismantles four specific starting beliefs (I cannot write / who am I / my story is not compelling / I cannot write well enough). None of the competitors above have publicly named this pattern. It gives Michele a differentiated positioning statement that reads as coach-native rather than marketing-native.

### 5. The authored body of work as case studies

Every other coach in the space depends on client testimonials. Michele's differentiation is that her own body of work stands in as the case study. She has done what she is coaching writers to do, twice, publicly. This is a durable authority signal none of the competitors match at the same depth.
