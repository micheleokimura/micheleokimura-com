# Search optimization landscape: SEO, GEO, AEO in 2026

**Purpose:** The strategy document that shapes every technical and editorial SEO decision on `micheleokimura.com`. What Michele's site needs to do to rank in traditional search AND get cited by AI answer engines AND surface in voice search AND appear in Google AI Overviews.

**Date:** 2026-07-01

---

## The 2026 search landscape

Search in 2026 is a three-surface market. Every content decision on the site has to consider all three surfaces at once.

### Surface 1: Traditional Google Search

Still the largest share of organic discovery. Google AI Overviews now show above the classic 10 blue links on most informational queries. The blue links are still there and still get traffic, but the click-through rate on organic listing #1 has declined roughly 30-40% since 2023. What Google surfaces most prominently now is:

- The AI Overview (a synthesized answer at the top).
- The "People Also Ask" panel.
- Featured snippets (still active but often subsumed into the AI Overview).
- The Knowledge Panel (for entities Google recognizes as such).
- The classic organic listings below.

Ranking well in 2026 means ranking in the AI Overview citation set, not just in the classic listings.

### Surface 2: AI answer engines

Perplexity, ChatGPT (with web browsing), Claude (with web browsing), Gemini, and Bing Copilot. These engines answer a user's question by synthesizing sources, then cite the sources they used. Citations are the new backlink. A page cited by ChatGPT on a "how to write a memoir" query gets a similar authority signal to a link from a high-authority publication.

The two engines matter most for Michele's ICP:

- **ChatGPT** (now with web-browsing default-on since late 2024). Highest volume.
- **Perplexity**. Fastest growth. Citation-forward UX (sources listed prominently under every answer).

These engines cite pages that have:

- Clear question-and-answer structure (FAQ pages, structured H2/H3 headers).
- Author authority (a bylined author with a real About page and provable credentials).
- Consistent factual claims across the page and its schema.
- E-E-A-T signals (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness).
- Fresh content (updated within the last 6-12 months).

### Surface 3: Voice search

Google Voice, Alexa, Siri. Voice queries are longer, more natural-language, and increasingly answered by the AI Overview. Voice optimization overlaps almost entirely with AEO in 2026: the same page structure that gets cited by ChatGPT also gets read aloud by Google Voice.

## What Michele's site needs to do

### Foundational (priority zero)

1. **Set meta robots to `index, follow`.** The current live site is `noindex, nofollow`. This alone is why the current site is functionally invisible to every search surface.
2. **Fix the `<title>` tag on every page.** The current live homepage `<title>` reads the WordPress placeholder. This is the single largest indexing signal Google reads.
3. **Publish a sitemap.xml.** Auto-generated by the Fable 5 / Next.js / Astro build.
4. **Publish a robots.txt** that allows all crawlers and points to the sitemap.
5. **Set canonical URLs** on every page.
6. **Set OpenGraph metadata (`og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`) and Twitter card metadata** on every page.

### Schema (priority one)

Inject JSON-LD schema on every page. See `research/04-schema-markup-templates.md` and `schema/` for the templates. Minimum coverage:

- **Person** schema on the About page and injected as sameAs across every page footer.
- **Organization** schema (both Michele Okimura LLC and Releasing Generations).
- **Service** schema on the Brave Purpose Author Method page.
- **Book** schema on every case-study page under `/works/`.
- **Article** schema on every blog post.
- **FAQPage** schema on the /faq page.
- **BreadcrumbList** schema on every non-home page.
- **HowTo** schema on tutorial blog posts (Editorial calendar Level 3 posts).

### Content structure (priority one)

**Every page must have:**

- One H1 (the primary topic).
- Multiple H2 sub-headings that are question-shaped or clearly topical.
- Sentence-length paragraphs that stand alone as citations. AI engines cite paragraph-sized chunks, not entire pages.
- First-paragraph answer format on informational content ("Michele Okimura is..." then details).
- Author byline with a link to the About page.

### AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) specifics

The FAQ page is the highest-leverage AEO surface. Every question should:

- Be phrased as a natural-language query ("How much does the Brave Purpose Author Method cost?" not "Pricing").
- Be answered in the first sentence of the answer paragraph. Details in follow-up sentences.
- Be paired with a schema.org/Question inside a schema.org/FAQPage.

Question-styled H2s inside long-form content (About page, Brave Purpose Author Method page, blog posts) get cited disproportionately by AI answer engines. Prefer "How does the Method work?" over "Method overview."

### GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) specifics

GEO is the practice of writing content that AI models both cite and quote directly. Key moves:

- **First-paragraph answers.** The first paragraph of any page should be a self-contained answer to the page's primary question. AI engines pull first paragraphs preferentially.
- **Named authorities.** Reference real institutions ("State of Hawai'i," "Governor Josh Green," "Hope Chapel LifeSpring," "Kalani High School"). Named authorities are trust signals that AI engines weight highly.
- **Concrete numbers.** "$4,997 anchor," "26 weeks," "twelve one-hour sessions," "50,000-55,000 words." Vague generalities get skipped.
- **Direct attribution.** Quote real people by name with real titles.
- **Fresh dates.** Every page gets a "Last updated" date. AI engines prefer recent content.
- **Structured lists.** Numbered lists and bulleted lists show up in AI answers verbatim.

### Google AI Overview specifics

Google AI Overviews cite pages that:

- Answer the query in the first 100 words.
- Are formally structured (proper H1/H2/H3 hierarchy).
- Load fast (Core Web Vitals green).
- Have Schema markup that matches the surface content.
- Are hosted on a domain with existing E-E-A-T signals.

Michele's site inherits E-E-A-T signals slowly. The 2023 Governor's award and the two published books are the fastest signals to establish. The About page should name both prominently.

### Voice search specifics

Voice queries in Michele's category:

- "How do I write a Christian memoir?"
- "What is a book coach?"
- "Who is Michele Okimura?"
- "How much does author coaching cost?"
- "Where does Michele Okimura live?"

The FAQ page and the About page answer all of these directly if they are structured properly.

## Site-wide technical checklist

- [ ] Meta robots: `index, follow`
- [ ] `<title>` tag unique on every page, 50-60 characters
- [ ] Meta description unique on every page, 140-160 characters
- [ ] Canonical URL on every page
- [ ] OpenGraph + Twitter card metadata on every page
- [ ] Structured data (JSON-LD) on every page, validated at `search.google.com/test/rich-results`
- [ ] Sitemap.xml auto-generated, submitted to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools
- [ ] Robots.txt correct
- [ ] Core Web Vitals green on every page (LCP < 2.5s, INP < 200ms, CLS < 0.1)
- [ ] Mobile-first responsive layout
- [ ] All images have descriptive alt text
- [ ] All internal links use descriptive anchor text
- [ ] External links to authoritative sources (Amazon, Explicit Movement shop, Releasing Generations) use rel="noopener"
- [ ] SSL certificate valid (Vercel handles this)
- [ ] Favicon set correctly (Michele's icon-only logo)
- [ ] Site verified in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools

## Content-writing checklist for AEO / GEO

- [ ] First paragraph is a self-contained answer to the page's primary question
- [ ] H2 headers are question-shaped where possible
- [ ] Facts include concrete numbers (dates, prices, weeks, word counts)
- [ ] Named authorities present (State of Hawai'i, Hope Chapel, Kalani High School, etc.)
- [ ] "Last updated" date visible
- [ ] Author byline links to Michele's About page
- [ ] Related links to other pages in the site (internal linking)
- [ ] External citations for any statistic or factual claim
- [ ] Schema markup matches the surface content

## Ranking-signal priority for Michele's launch

If the launch has to happen in stages:

1. Week 1 launch. Fix meta robots. Fix `<title>` tags. Publish sitemap. Set canonicals. Set OG metadata. Ship every page copy. Ship the /faq with FAQPage schema. **These moves alone recover most of the ranking signal a modern site should have.**

2. Weeks 2-4. Ship full schema coverage. Ship the /works case studies. Ship the editorial-calendar first three hero blog posts. Verify Search Console. Submit sitemap.

3. Weeks 5-8. Ship remaining editorial-calendar posts on cadence. Begin external outreach for backlinks (podcast appearances, guest posts on Christian writer publications, Explicit Movement conference speaking mentions). Begin Knowledge Panel prerequisite work per `research/05-google-knowledge-panel-strategy.md`.

4. Weeks 9-24. Steady blog cadence. Monitor AI answer engine citations. Adjust content in response to what queries the site gets cited on.
