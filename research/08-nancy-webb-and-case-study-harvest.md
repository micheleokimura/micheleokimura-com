Every case study file in `content/case-studies/` has a "To be harvested" section at the end listing content that has to come from Michele's inbox and other sources. This section is populated by a parallel Gmail research task.

### What the Gmail research task should do

For each authored work in `content/brand/michele-authored-works.md`, mine Michele's inbox (both `michele@micheleokimura.com` and `michele@releasinggenerations.org`) for:

1. **Reader letters and testimonials.** Search terms: the book title, "your book," "changed my life," "healed my heart," "thank you," combined with dates from publication year forward.
2. **Participation numbers.** Search for conference confirmation emails, workshop registration reports, session sign-in sheets in emails.
3. **Notable placements.** Search for school adoption confirmations, church curriculum orders, ministry partnership announcements.
4. **Endorsement text.** Search for pastor endorsements, foreword drafts, praise-page quotes.
