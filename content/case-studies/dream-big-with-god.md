---

## Origin story

Michele wrote *Brave Purpose with God* first. The faith edition was the testbed for the project. Chapters 9, 11, 12, 13, 14, and 15 were rebuilt from clean source under the strict "text-is-immutable" rules of engagement Michele has developed. The manuscript carries her Hope Chapel LifeSpring pastoral voice at its center, softened for readers across denominations rather than tied to any one lineage.

The secular edition (*Brave Purpose*) followed as a parallel adaptation. Both books release together in 2027.

---

## Structure and methodology

Identical structural arc to the secular edition: fifteen chapters, three Parts (Discovering the Dreamer Within / From Dreamer to Builder / Living Your Dream), free-verse chapter openings, Explorer's Notes, controlling metaphors sustained across chapters.

Where the faith edition differs:

- The Holy Spirit is named directly.
- Jesus, the Father, and Scripture are woven throughout.
- Faith moves that were removed in the secular edition are retained here: "a holy invitation," "the abundant life that Jesus offers," "dreams God has planted within you," "the King of Kings has made His home there."
- The About-the-Author closes with "a wife, a mother, and a Christ-follower."
- An Epilogue is included that may or may not appear in the secular edition (still an open editorial decision).

## What Michele learned that now feeds the Method

Writing the faith edition first, then adapting it to a secular edition, taught Michele how to hold a book's core teaching while shifting its register. The Method now teaches writers to name their register early ("who is this book for?") and to hold that register consistently through every chapter. When a writer needs to move a piece across registers (a book, a blog post, a talk), the same discipline applies.
