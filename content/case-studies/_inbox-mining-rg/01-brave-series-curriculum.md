## The Brave Series shape as of mid-2026

Three curriculum lines, each with 4 volumes and both a Classic (secular) and Faith edition. All published under Explicit Movement (the Releasing Generations initiative). Chief graphic designer: Sakura Reese (previously Ahava Design, `sakura.reese.creates@gmail.com`, 805-868-6941). Executive Assistant / production manager: Sarah Budd, based in England, dual signature `sarah@releasinggenerations.org` and `sarah@explicitmovement.org`. Additional design and brand contract help: TJ Russell (Sunbear Digital, `tj@sunbeardigital.com`, 208-994-2680).

**Brave & Beautiful** (for girls): 4 volumes, Classic and Faith. Faith Volume 1 reprint went to press May 2026 (100 copies, 75 to Michele and 25 to Sakura; 75 shipped from Hawaii to Philippines).

**Brave & Bold** (for boys): 4 volumes, Classic and Faith. All four volumes officially completed and announced 2026-05-19 (Michele's "We did it!" email — thread `19e400006c36e002`).

**Brave Together** (family / co-ed): 4 volumes, Classic and Faith. Faith versions still being edited and proofed through June-July 2026. Vol 1 Faith final proof approved 2026-06-19 (thread `19ee2c5b4c0e4166`). Volumes 2, 3, 4 Faith through final Sarah-Michele-Sakura edit loop June 2026.

Article contributors named across the series (partial list from thread traffic): Ben Klassen (writes separate Faith and secular versions), Dave Willweber (integrity article), Kahill Liddell, Lea (body image), Kanani (Building Bridges to a Closer Connection), JerriAnn Webb, Joshua (integrity), Dominique Roe (also does pre/post survey data analysis), Rachel Fischer, Abi.

Facilitator Guides for each series exist as digital add-ons (currently $7/each, $28 for 4-guide set, free with 5+ volume purchase per Sarah's Mar 2026 pricing model — thread `19cb944c1ca81d98`).

Digital flipbook platform: migrating from Issuu (which suddenly deleted their entire account in June 2026, thread `19f0051c2680fea8`) to Publuu in June-July 2026.
