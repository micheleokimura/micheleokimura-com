## Positioning

Michele's founding story of Explicit Movement and the core teaching that grew out of it. Memoir plus framework: how a small local youth conference in Hawai'i became a movement teaching parents, pastors, and youth workers to talk with teens about sex, sexual integrity, and healing.

---

## Origin story

In 2014 Michele ran her first island-wide youth and parent conference. She did not know it would grow into anything larger. By 2015 it had become a nonprofit organization with a middle school conference, a young adult conference, a parent conference, and a repeat of the original high school conference: four conferences in one year. By 2016 it had gone international, with conferences in Manila, Baguio, and Singapore. By 2018 the work had reached Southern California.

Michele wrote *The Birth of Explicit Movement* to name the pattern that had made this possible. Not the mechanics of running conferences, but the deeper method: how she and her team named a hard topic clearly, held it in a frame that combined truth with deep compassion, and made room for both parents and teens to enter the conversation. The book weaves her own story (from Hope Chapel LifeSpring, from the classroom, from the pastoral seat) with the framework that has now equipped Explicit Movement leaders across three continents.

## Structure and methodology

The book carries the same shape Michele now teaches in the Brave Purpose Author Method:

- **Origin, Call, Cost, Company.** The four beats of Act I. Michele's founding story of Explicit Movement follows this arc almost exactly. The Method now teaches this as a repeatable structure for any writer whose book carries a founding narrative.
- **Framework in Act II.** Michele's teaching on sexual integrity ("Real Love, Real Truth") sits inside Act II of the book. The Method teaches writers to move from Act I (their story) to Act II (their framework) with a clear turn.
- **Sending in Act III.** The book ends by handing tools to the reader. Every writer Michele now coaches learns to close a book with a sending. A closing chapter puts something in the reader's hands and points them at what to do next.

## What Michele learned that now feeds the Method

*The Birth of Explicit Movement* taught Michele that the memoir-plus-framework structure travels well. A reader will follow a story into a framework if the story is told honestly and the framework earns its place. The Brave Purpose Author Method uses this exact bridge: every writer's book moves from personal narrative into teachable pattern, and the transition has to feel earned. When Michele coaches a writer through the Discover-Develop-Deliver arc, she is walking the same road she walked to write this book.
