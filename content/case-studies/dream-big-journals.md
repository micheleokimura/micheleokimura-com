## Positioning

A curriculum of journals with teacher guides spanning preschool through adult editions. Activity-based, image-forward, and designed to work in schools, churches, and homes. The journals walk the reader from a first flicker of a dream to a step-by-step plan for making that dream real.

## Origin story

Michele's fourteen years in a Hawai'i public elementary classroom gave her a working sense of how children learn. Her more than twenty years as Associate Pastor and Children's Ministry lead at Hope Chapel LifeSpring gave her a working sense of how children think about God, purpose, and the wide open horizon of their own lives.

She spent a decade running Dream Big workshops for children, teens, and adults. Participants across every session asked the same question, in different words: "Can we do this at home?" or "Can we do this in my school?"

The Dream Big Journal Curriculum was Michele's answer. She wrote a journal for each developmental stage, backed each one with a teacher's guide, and gave every user (parent, teacher, or curriculum leader) the tools to run the framework without needing Michele in the room.

---

## Structure and methodology

Each edition is journal-plus-guide.

- **Journal.** Pages of prompts, activities, and image-driven exercises. Age-appropriate at every developmental stage.
- **Teacher guide.** Session-by-session flow, discussion prompts, and adaptable formats (single-session, weekly cadence, retreat).

The methodology that runs through the whole curriculum:

1. **Notice.** What is stirring inside you?
2. **Name.** What is that thing you cannot stop thinking about?
3. **Nurture.** What is the first step to bring it to life?
4. **Now walk it out.** What do you do this week?

The four-step arc is a compressed version of the Discover-Develop-Deliver structure Michele now teaches writers in the Brave Purpose Author Method.

## What Michele learned that now feeds the Method

The Journal Curriculum taught Michele that the same core question ("what is asking to be born through you?") lands differently at every life stage. A preschool page and an adult journal page ask the same working question, but the language, the imagery, and the pace shift. When Michele now coaches a writer, she teaches them to know their exact reader at the exact life stage, and to write for that reader without pulling the book flat across all audiences. Different books, different readers, different registers. Same underlying question.
