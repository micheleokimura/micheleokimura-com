## Origin story

Michele has been carrying this book for more than a decade. It grew out of her Dream Big Journal Curriculum, her Dream Big Framework (the fifteen-step methodology she teaches from stage and in workshops), and her own healing arc: forgot how to dream, believed her past defined her limits, learned that healing is the foundation for hope, and now works to help others remember. She wrote the faith edition first (*Brave Purpose with God*), then adapted the same manuscript in a parallel secular edition so the work could reach readers who are not walking a Christian faith. Both books release in 2027 under the same publishing imprint.

## Structure and methodology

Fifteen chapters in three Parts. The exact structure Michele's coaching engagement now teaches, ported into her own book:

- **Part 1: Discovering the Dreamer Within.** Chapters 1-6. The reader meets the writer's arc and learns to notice their own.
- **Part 2: From Dreamer to Builder.** Chapters 7-12. The reader learns the practices that turn a dream into a plan.
- **Part 3: Living Your Dream.** Chapters 13-15. The reader is sent.

Each chapter opens with a free-verse poem, an Explorer's Note (italic preview in second person), a story or a quote, and then the topic. This structure is now the "Explorer's Note" pattern in the Brave Purpose Author Method.

The book uses controlling metaphors that sustain across full chapters: treasure map, river, workshop, garden, closet, blueprint. Every writer Michele now coaches is taught to name a controlling metaphor early and sustain it.

## What Michele learned that now feeds the Method

Writing *Brave Purpose* in parallel with *Brave Purpose with God* taught Michele that a book's structure is more durable than any given register. The same fifteen-step arc holds whether the writer is naming the Holy Spirit or leaving faith framing out entirely. That insight shapes every engagement in the coaching program: the Method's structure is the container. The writer's voice, including the writer's faith register, is the content.
