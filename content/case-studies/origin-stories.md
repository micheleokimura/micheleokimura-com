## Dancing with Father (2011)

**Source file:** `~/My Drive (michele@micheleokimura.com)/PUBLICATIONS/Dancing with Father/DWF HARDCOPY BOOK/03_Print-Ready/DANCING WITH FATHER PDF.pdf` (pages ix-x, "INTRODUCTION")

I remember the incident as if it happened just yesterday. There I was, adorned in a beautiful white gown that mom had sewn for my Senior Prom. Like any typical teenage girl thrilled to be all dressed up and made up for such a distinctive occasion, I stood waiting in our living room with eager anticipation for my date to arrive. Feeling such joy and delight, the words came rolling off my tongue, "How do I look?"

The abrupt response hit me, taking my breath away as I gasped for air. "You look funny" instantly shot back at me in a critical and angry stare. The harsh retort only reinforced the message I had been receiving for years and what I already had come to believe about myself. That brief scene became a defining moment for me because in its brevity, it described a reality I lived in.

Sixteen years later, my husband and I attended a wonderful, week-long Christian conference that taught us how to facilitate God's healing to wounded souls. During lunch on the last day of the conference, the painful memory of my Senior Prom night came back to me. It caught me by surprise because I hadn't thought about the incident for a long time.

After lunch, we met in our assigned small groups as we had every day of the conference. Since this was our last meeting together, we took turns being prayed for by the rest of the group. I didn't tell them about the memory of my senior prom night, but as they prayed, one woman said she immediately had a vision of me in a white dress. Then another woman said that she also immediately saw a vision of me in a white dress while at a dance. A third woman excitedly shared that she too had a vision of me in a white dress, and that Father God wanted to dance with me.

Needless to say, I wept as I heard the Lord speak healing words to my heart regarding this painful memory. This was His new, defining moment for me.

Shortly after that wondrous encounter with Him, I was inspired to write *Dancing with Father*. The poem is a vision of my dance with Father God and a tribute to Him that testifies of how He healed me and gave me a new identity. My hope and prayer is that you also will be drawn into a personal encounter with Father God as you hear Him speak His transforming love to you.

All glory to Him who imparts a crown of beauty instead of ashes, the oil of joy instead of mourning and the garment of praise instead of a spirit of despair!

## The Birth of Explicit Movement (2018)

**Source file:** `~/My Drive (michele@micheleokimura.com)/Claude-Workspace/projects/clients/michele-okimura/04-manuscript/birth-of-explicit-movement/source-material/PRINT READY FINAL TheBirthOfExplicitMovement_Interior_6x9_6-18-18.pdf` (pages 15-16, "INTRODUCTION: THE FIRST THREE YEARS")

Welcome to the story about the birth of Explicit Movement, a surprising God idea! It is a unique story because it is unlike the birthing of many powerful and amazing ministries that were born out of a God-given passion or dream someone had and nurtured over time.

Let me describe the unusual Explicit Movement journey in this way. His assignment usually involved taking a step in a certain direction. I would keep walking, even though I was clueless to where I was going. Then, unexpectedly, there would be a turn in the road. As I would make the turn, God would surprise me with what was behind the bend, and my response would often be, "What Lord? You want to do that? No! I can't do that!" Following my initial resistance, there was often a painful process of complete surrender of my will for His will - a walk of obedience. I would then honor and trust His authority and wisdom, holding tight to His hand. I continued walking, never knowing what would happen next! Supernatural miracles, divine appointments, amazing favor, miracles of healed hearts and transformed lives filled the path as I walked. This describes what the first three years were like!

It is my hope that these testimonies will impart courage and strength to your heart to step out in faith and serve Him with increased abandon! May you encounter God. May the Holy Spirit speak to you encouraging messages from His heart to yours about your life and incredible purpose!

*"…for it is your Father's good pleasure to give you the kingdom."* - Luke 12:32 NKJV

## Brave Purpose (2027, secular edition)

**Source files:** `~/My Drive (michele@micheleokimura.com)/Claude-Workspace/Dream Big (Secular)/Typesetter Files/Brave Purpose Preface Typeset.docx` and `Brave Purpose Introduction Typeset.docx`

### Preface

Welcome to a great adventure.

The journey of dreaming big is a path I have walked for decades, and beautifully, it is the very road that has led me to you. As I have navigated the hills and valleys of discovering my true north, pursuing the dreams rooted within me with intention and openness, I have been left with a gratitude deeper than words can express. It has been a journey of personal transformation, profound healing, and experiencing the miraculous potential of life. This journey has gifted me with the kind of freedom that only comes from being willing to explore and learn what is in my own heart and to take steps to live with purpose.

Every milestone I have reached, from the hard-won victories to the quiet shifts of the soul, has led me to this desire: to see you embrace the power and richness of your own story. My passion and mission are to help you clear the path for you to realize the potential you carry and to dream with boldness. My heart is to help you navigate your way out of any shadows of self-doubt and the boundaries of external limitations so you can walk in the greater fullness of your true self.

There is a profound spark within you that is a quiet, persistent voice of potential that is far more than just a fleeting wish. It is an inner calling, a blueprint of what you are truly capable of.

You were meant to thrive, to live unhindered, vibrant, and deeply aligned with a sense of meaning. The dreams planted in your heart aren't accidental; they are there because you have something uniquely positive to offer this world. Together, let's unlock that treasure and watch as your life transforms into the masterpiece you were always meant to be.

May the brilliance of your true potential be witnessed and experienced through you, and through the impact of your dreams.

### Introduction - A Profound Invitation

Imagine, for a moment, that you are standing barefoot on a smooth, sun-warmed rock in the middle of a flowing river. The cool water swirls ankle-deep around you, and you can feel the gentle, persistent tug of flowing waters. It is inviting you to let go, to drift downstream into territory unknown, into a great adventure.

Yet, there is something comforting about the safety of the rock. It feels stable. It is familiar. It is known. It is like the somewhat predictable life you have created, and you know every inch of its surface.

But deeper than the comfort of where you stand, you feel it. A subtle, magnetic pull of a current toward something more. It is the call to experience deeper fulfillment, more radiant joy that makes your soul feel alive. And still, you may hesitate. It is uncomfortable, perhaps even frightening, to risk stepping off the solid ground of the "known" and into the flow of what could be.

This winding river journey is, at its heart, a pursuit of the hidden treasure chest of dreams planted within you. That is the voice calling out to you from the water.

You may wonder if the aspirations tucked away in the corners of your heart are too big, too impractical, or perhaps, simply too late. Dreams can feel like ancient history, long forgotten. Maybe the noise and relentless grind of daily life have silenced that inner voice. Maybe life doesn't seem as vibrant as it once did, and the weight of reality has hindered your desire to reach toward "boundless" possibilities. Or perhaps you've realized you simply don't know how to dream ... again.

#### The Invitation to the Deep: Your Flow in Every Season

Let's call this what it is: a profound invitation to step out of hiding and into your full potential.

Deep within the heart of every traveler, no matter the age, is a quiet, but persistent whisper that says, "You were not made for the stagnant shallows of just getting by." You were meant to explore the vastness of a life authored by your highest purpose. You are called to explore depths and enter into a breathtakingly beautiful journey that is often messy in its unfolding!

The truth is, our dreams breathe and shift with the seasons. A spark from your childhood may stay with you for a lifetime, while another may wait decades to be recovered. Consequently, the visions of your youth will naturally look different than those you hold in mid-life or your senior years. My deepest hope is that no matter your age or the season you find yourself in, you will live with a profound sense of unique purpose.

Whether you are just finding your footing, navigating the rush of midlife, or walking in the golden twilight of your retirement years, there are embers within you that are waiting to be ignited and reignited.

To help you navigate these shifting waters, let's look at the map of your current season:

- **To the Young Adult:** You are in the season of "The First Breath." Do not let the pressure to "become" stifle the unique spark of who you already are. Your dreams are not just about a career; they are about the crafting of your first courageous steps into what is possible.
- **To the Middle-Aged Adult:** You are in the season of "The Sustained Trek." Do not let the weight of responsibility turn your calling into a chore. It is time to move beyond merely surviving the daily grind to pursue an adventure aligned with your deepest values.
- **To the Senior:** You are in the season of "The Golden Legacy." Your purpose has not expired; it has matured. Even if your pace has slowed or your health has narrowed your physical world, your impact has never been more vital. Through your presence and seasoned wisdom, you release a depth of perspective and creativity that your peers and the younger generations need to hear.

Regardless of where you find yourself on the map of life, whether you are in a season of planting new seeds, building on established ground, or focusing on the legacy you wish to leave behind, the journey of discovery is calling. The masterpiece of your life is not a static image, but a story in motion.

The map is in your hands, the path ahead is deep with possibility, and the potential you've been carrying is ready to meet you exactly where you are.

#### The Map and the Movements

Your life is a treasure map. Your deepest passions are the compass. And even your most profound pains? They are not dead ends; they are the critical clues leading you straight to the "X." It's time to learn how to read your own story and start the courageous work of digging.

This journey is divided into three essential movements:

**Part One: Discovering the Dreamer Within.** This section is a reckoning of your worth. We begin your personal adventure by asking the necessary questions. For some of us, we may wrestle a bit until we fully recognize, embrace, and celebrate our immense value simply as human beings. We will walk, step by step, through the terrain of your life, dismantling the limiting beliefs and recognizing any underlying fear or shame you carry that may have kept you feeling small. You will finally use the map of your own experience to identify the unique, authentic dreams that are planted not outside of you, but right at the center of your purpose. We will move from the question of "What do I want?" To the more profound inquiry: "Who was I truly meant to be?"

**Part Two: From Dreamer to Builder.** Once the dream is identified, we trade the shovel for the drafting tools. This section moves us from initial discovery to faithful architecture. We will explore practical, psychological tools: learning to partner with your potential using your imagination as a vital blueprint, reclaiming the powerful engine of your innate creativity. From there, we will learn the discipline of effectively managing the "three T's" you already hold in your hands: your Time, your Treasure (resources), and your Talents. You cannot build a dream without the courage to manage what you already possess.

**Part Three: Achieving and Living Your Dream.** Let's be honest about the journey ahead: A dream, once uncovered and given a name, becomes vulnerable. It will be tested. You will be tested. This is the wilderness where countless dreams are abandoned, and countless visionaries get stuck. This book will not leave you stranded there. You will gain enduring keys to continue your journey: showing you how to build the inner fortitude and resilience to protect your vision when the temptation to quit is loudest, and how to find your tribe, the supportive community that will champion your purpose when you simply can't champion yourself.

#### The Pace of the Trek

Before we dive into these movements, I want to offer a gentle word of counsel for the journey ahead. Because this is a practical navigation guide, it is not a quick read. We are doing the deep, transformative work of digging for treasure that has been buried for years.

I encourage you to resist the urge to rush. To get the most out of this experience, treat it like a marathon of discovery rather than a sprint to the finish. Linger over the Explorer's Notes, sit with any new perspectives, and allow the Treasure Summaries to settle deep into your heart. Your breakthrough may be just one day away, but it is the steady, patient steps that ensure you arrive at your destination refreshed and ready to lead a life of "More!"

#### The Rhythm of Yes

The startling truth is that the most valuable treasure is not found on distant shores; it is something you must discover, uncover, and sometimes, recover within you. It is the limitless potential already woven into your being.

May you hear the whispers of your own intuition asking, "What if?" and "Why not?" Those tiny sparks of wonder that invite you back to the dreaming. When you answer those whispers, you begin to establish a new heartbeat for your life: the Rhythm of Yes. It is choosing growth over comfort, again and again. With brave breath, let us chase those answers. This is not a book of "nice wishes"; it is a purpose-fueled navigation guide designed to move you beyond a comfortable "bucket list" and into a daring adventure of expanding joy, profound meaning, and boundless possibility.

You may feel like you aren't ready, but everything you need to begin is already within you.

#### Redefining "Big"

As we begin, let's redefine what it means to "Dream Big." For some, a big dream involves a statewide business or a worldwide platform. But for others, the biggest dream imaginable is to profoundly impact a small group of people up close and personal.

Remember: You can impress people from afar, but you can only have a deep impact on people up close.

If your dream is to reach out to the lonely one life at a time, one "starfish" at a time, that is as noble and significant a mission as starting a global corporation. Some of us are naturally wired for the profound power of 1:1 impact. In a life of purpose, a dream isn't measured by the size of the audience, but by the depth of the commitment and the heart behind the action.

Dreaming "bigger" doesn't always mean dreaming wider; sometimes it means dreaming deeper. For example:

- Instead of just "visiting the elderly," your bigger dream may be to restore the dignity of specific seniors by becoming a consistent source of genuine friendship.
- Instead of just "mentoring a student," your bigger dream might be to break a generational cycle of discouragement in one child's life.

Don't let the "loudness" of worldwide influence drown out the "still, small voice" of your specific path. Whether your treasure is meant to be a lighthouse for a city or a lamp in a single room, it is meant to shine. Your path is noble, necessary, and vital.

With that truth as your compass, it's time to step off the rock of familiarity and become a voyager. As you enter the flow of the journey, you will find that new worlds, even strange and wonderful ones, unfold on every side. Upon these dancing waters, whether you find a peaceful current or brave the rapids, you will discover territory that will take you by delightful surprise.

It is time to trust the process and find your flow. Let us embrace childlike curiosity and allow the river of possibility to take us on the adventure of a lifetime.

The current is moving. It's time to Dream Big!
