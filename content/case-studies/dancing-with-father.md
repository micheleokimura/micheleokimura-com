## Positioning

An illustrated poetic book of healing for women. A short, image-forward invitation to see life as a dance with Father God, one that can restore what was lost and make whole what was shattered.

---

## Origin story

Michele wrote *Dancing with Father* in the wake of a healing moment she describes on the current site's blog in a post called "The Great Dance." A small group of new friends prayed over her, sharing a vision of Father God inviting her to dance in the dance of life. The prayer released a memory Michele had carried since her senior prom, when a critical word had reinforced an earlier belief that she was not valued. The healing that followed dissolved rejection into affirmation. She was, she came to see, a Chosen One.

Shortly after that healing moment, Michele wrote the poem *Dancing with Father*. It described a wonder-filled journey of freedom and newfound purpose that came from a Father God who heals. The poem became a short book. The short book found its way, by hand and by mail, into the lives of women across Norway, Japan, the Philippines, and back home in Hawai'i.

One reader once approached Michele in person to say the book had reached her the night she nearly took her own life. "Your book saved my life," she said, through tears. That story sits in Michele's memory as the reason the book existed at all.

## Structure and methodology

*Dancing with Father* is short by design. It is a poem more than a treatise. Michele set the healing story inside imagery a reader could sit with: a dance, a Father, a partner leading well. The book pairs Michele's poetic verse with Danielle Iranon's paintings, so the imagery lives both in the words and on the page.

The structural moves Michele used here later became patterns in the Brave Purpose Author Method:

- **Story as spine.** The book does not open with a thesis. It opens with a memory (the prom, the critical word) and lets the healing unfold as narrative before it lands as teaching.
- **Image-first.** Every emotional beat has a visual pair. The prom dress, the closet, the dance, the Father. The Method now teaches writers to name a controlling image early and let it carry the chapter.
- **Compact.** *Dancing with Father* is not a long book. Michele has since learned to trust that some books are supposed to be short. The Method sizes each engagement to the book that is actually asking to be written.
- **Testimony as source material.** The book began with a personal experience Michele had lived. The voice-corpus method used in the coaching engagement carries the same principle forward: the writer's own lived experience is the primary source material, and everything is built from there.

## What Michele learned that now feeds the Method

*Dancing with Father* taught Michele that a book does not have to be long to change a life. What it has to be is honest. The story of a healing has to sound like the healing actually felt. Every writer she now coaches learns this same principle inside Week 2 of the Method: the voice on the page has to be honest to the writer's voice off the page. When that lines up, the reader knows.
