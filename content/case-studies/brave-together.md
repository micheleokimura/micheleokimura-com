## Positioning

The largest and most-adopted volume in the Brave Series. Designed for coed classrooms, youth groups, and mixed-audience settings where a single curriculum needs to serve boys and girls together. Built on the same four working ideas that run through every Brave Series product: unlock purpose, prevent exploitation, establish identity and self-worth, build resiliency, confidence, and skills to live a meaningful life.

---

## Origin story

Michele wrote *Brave & Beautiful* for teen girls first. The immediate follow-on request from schools and ministries was for a boys' edition, and *Brave & Bold* answered that request. What came next was a room-level need she heard from every facilitator using the two: many teen ministries and school programs mix girls and boys in the same lesson block, and running two curricula in parallel is not practical.

*Brave Together* was Michele's answer. It ports the Brave Series framework into a coed lesson set that a facilitator can deliver in one room, on one calendar, with one lesson plan. Every lesson holds the same story-and-activity structure the earlier volumes proved out.

---

## Structure and methodology

- 80+ lessons, each with a companion facilitator slide deck.
- Modular delivery: single-day retreat, weekly small group, semester elective, or embedded into an existing youth program.
- Each lesson pairs a story-anchored teaching with a hands-on activity and a discussion path that works for coed groups.
- Facilitator fidelity is protected. Michele requires a live orientation zoom with any new facilitator or partner site before piloting begins. Adoption is not simply "download and go."

The pattern that connects this curriculum to the Brave Purpose Author Method: Michele architects a body of teaching as a set of chapters that stand alone AND cohere as a whole. Every writer she coaches learns this two-level design, individual lesson integrity plus whole-body coherence.

## Reach and impact

*Brave Together* is the most internationally adopted volume in the Brave Series to date.

### The Philippines

Beth Potter of Pag-Asa Foundation and TeachBeyond began piloting *Brave Together* with vulnerable Filipino students in late 2025 after an introduction from Tricia Steeper. Michele's team gave Beth free online access to the full series for review. Michele held teacher-training zooms across Hawai'i and Philippines time zones through the first quarter of 2026. Michele's fidelity rule held: no piloting with students until a live orientation zoom happened first.

### Brazil

Colégio El Shadai (São João da Boa Vista, Brazil) is preparing to bring Michele and co-presenter Phyllis on-site for teacher training in 2026. Point person: Tiago Campos, School Director. Planning and translation logistics have been running since February 2026.

### Hawai'i

*Brave Together* is in use across schools, churches, and youth ministries in Hawai'i alongside the earlier Brave Series volumes. The 2023 State of Hawai'i Outstanding Advocate for Children and Youth recognition (awarded to Explicit Movement, the umbrella of the Brave Series) surfaced the work to educators, principals, and complex-area leaders across the state.

### The mainland U.S.

Facilitator sites in California, Missouri, and other states use *Brave Together* in youth ministry and after-school settings. The curriculum is licensed rather than distributed openly to protect fidelity.

## What Michele learned that now feeds the Method

Two lessons from *Brave Together* now shape every Brave Purpose Author Method engagement.

First, the discipline of coed voice. Writing a single lesson that lands the same way for a fifteen-year-old boy as it does for a fifteen-year-old girl is a voice-and-register problem. Same content lands differently in the two ears. The Method now teaches writers to name their reader early and to test every chapter against that reader.

Second, the discipline of facilitator fidelity. Michele will not ship a curriculum without a live orientation call with the facilitator. The Method carries the same posture. The writer being coached is not shipping their book alone. There is a live handoff at every stage.
