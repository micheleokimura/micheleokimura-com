## Notes on the "brave purpose" tagline

The current live site uses "brave purpose. If I can DREAM BIG so can you." That tagline still holds and can be retained as a small support line under the hero. It is the master brand phrase Michele has used since 2025.

Recommended treatment on the new site: keep "brave purpose" as the master line, drop the "If I can DREAM BIG so can you" as a secondary line only. The full tagline reads dated in 2026; the phrase "brave purpose" alone reads timeless.
