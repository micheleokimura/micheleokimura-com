## Section 1: The four rooms

### Author.

Two published trade books so far. Two more releasing in 2027 under the shared title *Brave Purpose*. In between, a teen leadership curriculum used in schools and youth ministries across three continents, a journal curriculum spanning preschool through adult editions, and a lesson book for parents raising children of faith. I write for the reader who is carrying something worth putting on the page and is not sure how to begin.

### Speaker.

I teach on dreaming, purpose, healing, creativity, and the courage it takes to raise the next generation. I have taught these ideas from a public school classroom on O'ahu, from a church pulpit in Honolulu, from conference stages in Manila and Baguio and Singapore, and from a Southern California ballroom. The message shifts to the room. The core stays the same.

### Executive Director.

Releasing Generations is the Christian nonprofit my husband Rob and I helped bring into the world. I am its founder and Executive Director. Three initiatives sit under it: Explicit Movement, Kingdom Families, and ReThink Creativity. The Brave Series teen curriculum lives there too. In 2023 the State of Hawai'i named Explicit Movement its Outstanding Advocate for Children and Youth. Governor Josh Green and Mayor Rick Blangiardi handed me that award at the 30th anniversary of Hawai'i Children and Youth Day. I still cannot believe we were even nominated.

### Coach.

The Brave Purpose Author Method is the six-month coaching engagement I run out of Michele Okimura LLC. I walk one writer at a time through the same book structure I use for my own manuscripts. Blank page, half-drafted, or almost-finished, the Method holds the shape while the writer's voice does the work.

## Section 2: My story

I forgot how to dream for a long time.

I was born in Honolulu in 1962. I grew up in Kaimuki as the oldest of three sisters. My parents worked hard. Our home was safe on the outside. Inside my own head I heard, very early and very consistently, that I did not measure up. There is a memory I keep close: a four-year-old girl hiding in the safety of a dark closet, feeling unloved, uncertain, and lost. That girl was me.

Slowly, and I do mean slowly, that girl grew into a woman who believed she had worth. Healing came first. Everything else came from there.

### Fourteen years in a classroom.

I graduated from Kalani High School in 1979 (Miss Teen USA finalist, and no, I didn't win, but I loved the experience). I finished a bachelor's in Elementary Education from the University of Hawai'i at Manoa in 1984. That same year I married Rob Okimura after a three-year courtship. He is still my best friend and the love of my life.

For roughly fourteen years I taught elementary school in the Hawai'i public system. Those were formative years. What I know about how a person actually learns, I learned in a classroom of eight-year-olds.

### Pastoring alongside my husband.

In 1997 Rob and I planted the church in Honolulu that is now called Lifespring Church (originally Hope Chapel LifeSpring). I have been on staff there ever since, mostly overseeing children's ministry and then youth ministry. I still serve as a part-time pastor at Lifespring today, alongside our lead pastors Sean and Hitomi Nakamoto.

I did not call myself a pastor for the longest time. A phone call with a friend named Steve, followed by an unrelated Jim Carrey interview on the Oprah Winfrey Show that afternoon, both delivering the exact same sentence in the exact same words ("Michele, if God has given you that mantle, you must take it and wear it"), changed that in an afternoon. I wrote the whole story down in the Wisdom Flows post called "The Mantle." Read it if you want the long version.

### The books.

I published *Dancing with Father* in 2011. It is a short, illustrated book of healing for women. I wrote it after a small group of new friends prayed over me one afternoon and I heard Father God inviting me to dance with Him in the dance of life. That book has ended up in the hands of women in Norway and Japan and the Philippines. A stranger once told me my book had reached her the night she almost took her own life. She chose life instead.

I published *The Birth of Explicit Movement* in 2018, along with a 21-day interactive journal. That book tells the founding story of Explicit Movement and lays out the framework we teach parents, pastors, and youth workers about talking with teens about sex, sexual integrity, and healing.

I have been writing the two Brave Purpose books for the last three years. The secular edition and the faith edition share a structure and much of the same prose. The secular edition strips explicit theological framing and reads for anyone; the faith edition names the Holy Spirit and Jesus and Scripture directly. Both release in 2027. Advance endorsements are in hand from a slate of Christian and educational leaders, including Focus on the Family, Missio Nexus, Ministry Leader Network, thinkSMALL Foundation, and Island Pacific Academy.

### The curricula.

The Brave Series (Brave & Beautiful, Brave & Bold, Brave Together), the Dream Big Journal Curriculum, the Raising Kingdom Kids Lesson Book. Different formats and different age brackets. The same working question is underneath every one: what if you were made for something big enough to matter, and how do you find your way in.

## Section 3: What I do now

I write. I speak. I run Releasing Generations. And I coach one writer at a time through the Brave Purpose Author Method.

If you are the reader on the other side of this page, here is what to know. I sound like this because I have lived it. I write in second-person because I am talking to you. I teach frameworks because they hold the shape while your voice does the work. I do not push. I invite.

The four rooms take different shapes for different readers.

- If you carry a book, come through the coaching door.
- If you lead an organization, a team, a classroom, or a ministry and want me to speak into it, come through the speaking door.
- If you want to read the work first, start with the books.
- If Explicit Movement or Kingdom Families or the Brave Series is what you searched for, visit Releasing Generations.
