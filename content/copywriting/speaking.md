## Section 1: What Michele teaches

Six evergreen talks. Each one has been delivered in a Hawai'i public school, on a church platform, at an international conference, or in a boardroom.

### 1. Dream Big. (Signature keynote.)

Michele's signature keynote. The 15-step framework that runs through her two forthcoming books and her Dream Big Journal Curriculum. For any audience across faith and secular lines. Adaptable to teachers, teens, leadership teams, parents, or a general audience.

### 2. Brave Purpose.

For the room asking "what is my purpose?" and needing a story-anchored answer that lands. Draws on Michele's Brave Purpose books, the four-beat arc (forgot, past defined me, healing, purpose), and her own story of moving from a four-year-old hiding in the closet to a woman standing in front of a Governor accepting an award she didn't know she was up for.

### 3. Raise, Restore, Release.

The three verbs of Releasing Generations. For educators, youth workers, parents, and ministry leaders. What it means to raise a child, restore a wounded heart, and release a dreamer into purpose.

### 4. The Explicit Movement talk.

Frank, faith-anchored teaching on sexual integrity, healing from abuse, and equipping the next generation. For parents, pastors, youth workers, and school staff who want a "real love, real truth" frame.

### 5. Reawaken your creativity.

Based on Michele's conviction that "you were created by the Creator to create." For leadership teams, teachers, and anyone whose creative gift has gone quiet. Michele hosted online Creativity Conferences in 2020 and 2021 with global reach; this talk is the compressed keynote version.

### 6. Brave Heart, Dream On.

Michele's closing keynote from the Dream Big Conclusion. Anaphora-driven. Benediction-cadence. For end-of-conference send-offs, graduation ceremonies, and any room that needs a room-filling closing note.
