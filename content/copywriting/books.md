## Opening paragraph

Michele has been putting brave purpose on the page for more than fourteen years. She writes across two registers (faith and secular), across many age brackets (preschool through adult), and across two forms (trade books and curriculum). What holds them together is a method she has field-tested twice on her own books and now teaches as The Brave Purpose Author Method. Every title below is a case study of that method at work. Click any cover to read the full case study: origin, structure, reach, and what she learned that now feeds the coaching room.

## Trade books (published)

### Dancing with Father (2011, Xulon Press)

Illustrated poetic book of healing for women. Paintings by Danielle Iranon. Started as a poem written in the wake of a prayer meeting in which a small group of women prayed a vision of Father God inviting Michele to dance with Him in the dance of life. That poem became a book that has reached women from Norway to Japan to the Philippines. One reader told Michele the book kept her alive on a night she nearly took her own life.

## Curricula

### The Brave Series (2022-present)

The flagship teen leadership curriculum from Releasing Generations. Three editions. Michele served as Creative Director, Chief Editor, and lead author.

- **Brave & Beautiful.** For teen girls. Focused on identity, worth, and protection from exploitation.
- **Brave & Bold.** For teen boys. Focused on courage, healthy masculinity, and purpose.
- **Brave Together.** Coed. 80+ lessons with slide decks. Focused on shared identity and mission.

Used in schools, churches, and nonprofits across Hawai'i and beyond. Featured on `braveseries.square.site` and `braveandbeautiful.world`.
