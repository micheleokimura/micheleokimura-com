## Section 1: The Brave Purpose Author Method

### Do I need to be a Christian to work with you?

No. The Method holds for any book. If you are writing in a secular voice, I coach you in that voice. If you are writing for a Christian reader, I coach you in that one. I speak credibly across both audiences because my own two forthcoming books release in parallel secular and faith editions.

### Do I need to have a draft already?

No. Many writers come in with an idea, a paragraph of notes, or a half-finished draft from years ago. The Method meets you in whichever posture you are in. Three postures: blank page, partial draft, finished manuscript that needs voice. Same fee, same 26 weeks, same weekly rhythm.

### What if I already have a finished manuscript?

Then the Method reads your draft against the Brave Purpose Book Structure, names what belongs where, and walks you through the voice pass that most self-edited manuscripts need. If you are on your second or third book and want this one to feel different, that is exactly the posture the Method serves.

### How long is the engagement?

Twenty-six weeks from signature. Many writers finish earlier. The term is an outer wall so we are not rushed.

### What is the price?

$4,997 for the full engagement. Two-payment or six-payment plans available. Hawai'i General Excise Tax may apply.

### What does the fee include?

The Method running weekly, up to twelve one-hour live Zoom sessions across the term, written or recorded review on every submission, AI-assisted developmental editing, three AI cover concepts by Week 22, a Google Drive shared folder, a private GitHub backup transferred to you at Week 26, the Mid-engagement Checkpoint at Week 13, the Session 26 delivery, and post-engagement follow-up notes at day 7, 30, 90, and 180.

### What does the fee not include?

Therapy or clinical counseling. Ghostwriting credit. A guaranteed publishing deal. Marketing campaigns. Third-party fees (books.by subscription, publisher fees). ISBN purchase or registration. Coaching after Week 26. See the coaching page for the full list.

### How is this different from a regular book coach?

A traditional book coach works on a manual timeline that can take years. The Brave Purpose Author Method folds Claude and Gemini into the workflow so writing moves faster without flattening your voice. You still get coached on structure, story, and prose. You also walk away knowing how to use the tools that are reshaping the craft.

### Will my book sound like AI?

If you follow the Method, your book will sound like you. The voice corpus is the whole point. Your own spoken and written voice becomes the source material Claude and Gemini draw from. By the end of the engagement you will be able to tell within two sentences whether a draft sounds like you. So will your readers.

### What if I miss a week?

Sessions are scheduled in advance and can be rescheduled with reasonable notice. Nothing is lost. The weekly packet still lands on Friday. The written or recorded review still goes back. We find a live-session time that works.

### What happens at Week 26?

You leave with a manuscript, three AI cover concepts to choose from, and a walk through three publishing paths in writing: Legacy Publishing (a hybrid publisher I refer to), books.by (self-publishing), or independent (KDP, IngramSpark, or a hybrid of your choosing). The choice is yours.

### Do you have testimonials from past coaching clients?

The Brave Purpose Author Method is new. Nancy Webb is client #1. Rather than wait for polished after-the-fact testimonials, I show in-process case studies of current engagements on the coaching page, with the writers' signed consent. The deeper authority stack is my own body of authored work; every book, curriculum, and workbook I have written is a case study of the same method now on offer to you.

### Can I see a sample weekly packet?

Send me an email at `michele@micheleokimura.com` with the subject "sample weekly packet" and I will send you a redacted example.
