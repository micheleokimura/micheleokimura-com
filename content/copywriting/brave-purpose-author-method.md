## Section 2: Who this is for {#three-postures}

Three engagement postures. Same Method, same weekly rhythm, same anchor price.

### The blank page.

You have carried this book for years. You have not yet had the structure, the time, or the company to begin. We start where every writer starts. We find the shape together. We build the voice corpus from the first conversation. By the end of the first month, chapter one has its opening scene on the page.

### The partial draft.

Chapters and scenes and notes and outlines in different folders, in different stages. Some of them you love. Some of them you do not know what to do with. We map what you have against the structure. We name what belongs where. We finish the drafting together, week by week, until the book is on the page.

### The finished manuscript.

You have already done the hard work of getting words down. What you need is someone to read it like a coach reads it, hold it against a structure that protects what is good, and walk with you while you tune the voice and finish the edit. Some of you are coming off a previous book and want this one to feel different. The Method makes room for that.

### This is not for you if.

You are looking for a developmental editor only, a ghostwriter to write the book in someone else's voice, a guaranteed publishing deal, a marketing campaign, or therapy. I am not a licensed counselor and I refer out when the work asks for it.

## Section 3: The Method {#the-method}

Twelve chapters in three Acts. Four chapters per Act. Around 4,000 to 4,500 words per chapter. A finished manuscript between 50,000 and 55,000 words, sized for the Christian living trade shelf or any adjacent shelf your book sits on.

**Act I: Discover.** The reader meets the writer. Origin, the Call, the Cost, the Company.

**Act II: Develop.** The reader meets the framework. The Framework, the Practice, the Proof, the Patterns.

**Act III: Deliver.** The reader is sent. The First Step, the Obstacles, the Long Haul, the Benediction.

### The weekly rhythm.

Predictable on purpose. Friday packet from me lands in your inbox. Tuesday or Wednesday Zoom session on the weeks you use a live session (up to twelve across the term, no minimum, no penalty for using fewer). Saturday and Sunday homework, dictated and written. Friday submission back to me. Weekend integration by me. Monday next packet.

### The voice corpus.

Your spoken and written voice samples become the source material. Session transcripts, Wispr Flow dictations, prior writing. Claude and Gemini work from that corpus so drafts come back sounding like you. The output is your voice, drawn from your own words.

### The structural-reminder pattern.

When you want to chase a thread off the week's chapter, I gently remind you that the structure is the scaffold that lets your voice land. By chapter five or six, most writers are bending it on purpose. That is the whole point.

## Section 11: Frequently asked questions

The full FAQ lives at `/faq`. A short version here for the writers who need to answer their first question before booking.

**Do I need to be a Christian to work with Michele?**

No. The Method holds for any book. If you are writing in a secular voice, Michele coaches in that voice. If you are writing for a Christian reader, she coaches in that one. She speaks credibly across both audiences.

**Do I need to have a draft?**

No. Many writers come in with an idea, a paragraph of notes, or a half-finished draft from years ago. The Method meets you in whichever posture you are in.

**What if I miss a week?**

Sessions are scheduled in advance and can be rescheduled with reasonable notice. Nothing is lost.

**What is the outer wall on time?**

Twenty-six weeks from signature. Many writers finish earlier. The term is an outer wall so the work is not rushed.

**What happens at Week 26?**

You leave with a manuscript, three AI cover concepts, and a walk through three publishing paths (Legacy Publishing, books.by, or independent). The choice is yours. Michele holds no promise on publisher acceptance, book sales, or bestseller outcomes.
