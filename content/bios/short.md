# Short bio (~60 words)

**Use:** About block on a program page, podcast guest bio, event program blurb, LinkedIn intro.

---

Michele Okimura is an author, speaker, and coach based on O'ahu, Hawai'i. She is the founder of Michele Okimura LLC and the founder and Executive Director of Releasing Generations. Across her ongoing pastoral work at Lifespring Church in Honolulu (the church she and her husband Rob planted in 1997), fourteen years in Hawai'i public school classrooms, and two published trade books with two more on the way, she has learned what it takes to turn the book in someone to the book on the page.
