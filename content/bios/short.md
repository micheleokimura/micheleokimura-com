# Short bio (~60 words)

**Use:** About block on a program page, podcast guest bio, event program blurb, LinkedIn intro.

---

Michele Okimura is an author, speaker, and coach based on O'ahu, Hawai'i. She is the founder of Michele Okimura LLC and the founder and Executive Director of Releasing Generations. Across more than twenty years pastoring at Hope Chapel LifeSpring alongside her husband Rob, fourteen years in Hawai'i public school classrooms, and two published trade books with two more on the way, she has learned what it takes to turn the book in someone to the book on the page.
