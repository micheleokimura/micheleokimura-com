Michele Okimura is an author, speaker, workshop leader, and coach based on O'ahu, Hawai'i. She is the founder of Michele Okimura LLC, the commercial home of her workshops, retreats, coaching, conferences, the Wisdom Flows blog, the Brave Purpose trade books, and the Dream Big Journals (preschool through adult editions). She is also the founder and Executive Director of Releasing Generations, a Christian nonprofit she runs as a separate vehicle. In 2023 the State of Hawai'i named her the Outstanding Advocate for Children and Youth.

Honolulu-born, Michele spent roughly fourteen years as a Hawai'i public school teacher and is a part-time pastor at Lifespring Church in Honolulu, the church she and her husband Rob planted in 1997 (originally as Hope Chapel LifeSpring). She has published two trade books and has two more in production. Her central methodology, the Dream Big Framework, runs across her speaking, her workshops, and the Brave Purpose Author Method, the six-month coaching engagement she runs out of the LLC.
