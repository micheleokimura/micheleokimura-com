# Brave Series Square shop inventory

Scraped from the live Square shop at https://braveseries.square.site/s/shop on 2026-08-22.

Source of truth for the Brave Series case-study breakout pages. Every product listed here has a cover image saved under `public/images/brave-series/`.

## How to read this

- **Edition.** The shop labels the two editions **Faith** and **Classic**. Classic is the non-faith (secular) edition.
- **Format.** Each title ships as DIGITAL ACCESS or HARD COPY. Facilitator guides and curriculum bundles are digital only.
- **Image reuse.** 90 products share 70 distinct cover images. Where two products use the same artwork (most often the Faith and Classic digital editions of one volume, or a volume and its curriculum bundle), the file is saved once per product under its own name so each page can reference a stable path.
- **Image source.** Files were pulled from the Square/Weebly CDN at original resolution (no `?width=` parameter), ranging from 1000px to 2000px on the long edge.

**Totals.** 90 products, 70 distinct images, 90 files saved.
