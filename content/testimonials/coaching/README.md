## Testimonial-harvesting prompts (Section 6 of the brand architecture doc)

At day 90 post-engagement, Michele sends three prompts to the client:

1. Before we started, what had gone quiet?
2. What changed in the middle of the work?
3. What did you walk into after?

Three short paragraphs. Together they tell Michele's four-beat arc from the client's perspective.
