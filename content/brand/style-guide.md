# Style guide (editorial + typographic)

**Scope:** Formatting and typography rules for every published surface on `micheleokimura.com`.

---

## Headings

- **H1.** One per page. Sentence case. The page's primary topic.
- **H2.** Section headers. Sentence case.
- **H3.** Sub-section headers within a section. Sentence case.
- **H4-H6.** Use sparingly. Sentence case.

Do not use title case except on proper-noun program elements: *The Brave Purpose Author Method*, *Mid-engagement Checkpoint*, *Writing Assignment #12*.

## Body copy

- Prefer paragraphs to bullets in prose. Bullets are fine for what-you-get lists.
- Sentences short to medium. Vary rhythm. A short percussive sentence after a longer setup sentence is a signature Michele move.
- Address the reader as "you." Michele writes second person.
- Never open a paragraph with a stack of standalone questions.

## Lists

- Bulleted list: use for parallel items. Each item stands alone. Cap at 7 items unless the list is a full catalog.
- Numbered list: use only when order matters.
- Nested lists: allowed, one level deep, no deeper.

## Blockquotes

Use for pull quotes and endorsements. Include attribution below the quote, on its own line.

## Links

- Internal links: relative paths (e.g., `/coaching/the-brave-purpose-author-method`).
- External links: full https URL. Open in the same tab by default.
- Link text should describe what the reader will land on. Not "click here."

## Images

- Every image needs alt text. Descriptive, not decorative.
- Prefer WebP over JPEG or PNG for photos. Fallback JPEG for older browsers.
- Fixed aspect ratios: 3:2 for stage photos, 1:1 for headshots, 4:3 for book covers.

## Dates

- Long form in body prose: October 1, 2023.
- Short form in metadata: 2023-10-01 (ISO 8601).
- Do not use MM/DD/YYYY.

## Times

- Short form: 3 pm HST (with space).
- Long form: 3:00 p.m. Hawai'i Standard Time.

## Money

- $4,997 (comma-separated, dollar sign).
- Never $5,000 or "about $5K."

## Emphasis

- **Bold** for the first appearance of a proper-noun program element.
- *Italic* for book titles, journal titles, and film titles.
- Do not use ALL CAPS in body copy except for logotype (e.g., `MOKimura_Logo`).

## Do not use

- Em dashes.
- Decorative emojis.
- Marketing-bro exclamations (!!!, all-caps HYPE, or "!!!!").
- Trailing ellipses to suggest mystery (...).
